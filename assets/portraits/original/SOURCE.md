Original Microsoft Fluent flat portrait vectors. Source commit: 1ffb34c752ecf5d402f04cfb4b392c77f57c54bc
https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji
MIT license in LICENSE.txt. These are the matching Windows portrait shapes, not a new face design.

Lifespan coverage uses the same pinned source's Baby, Boy, Girl, Child, Old man,
Old woman and Older person flat assets in all six original tones. Neutral faces
without personal colours/details continue to use the original glyph renderer.
Expression changes transform only the existing mouth, eyes and brows; head,
nose, ears, wrinkles and hair geometry are retained. Colour/accessory choices
are saved on the actual person and work throughout ordinary childhood/adult/
older-person appearances. Special outfit and legacy adult hair glyphs continue
to use their established renderer until their exact matching paths are covered.
