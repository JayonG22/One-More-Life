# ONE MORE LIFE — Master Art Style

## Goal
A compact, hand-authored-looking life-sim icon language: friendly, readable, restrained, and recognisable at 24 px.

## Anti-“AI gloss” rules
- No glossy plastic highlights.
- No airbrushed gradients.
- No halo glow unless the icon literally represents magic/light.
- No decorative sparkle unless it carries meaning.
- No hyper-rounded 3D sticker look.
- No micro-textures or tiny decorative marks that vanish below 48 px.
- No perfectly mirrored geometry when a slight asymmetry reads better.

## Core construction
- Canvas: 256×256 transparent; portraits 512×512.
- Safe area: 28 px on 256 assets; 54 px on 512 portraits.
- Master outline: warm charcoal, 10–12 px at 256, rounded caps and joins.
- Internal detail lines: 6–8 px.
- Maximum 3 primary fill colours per normal icon, plus outline.
- Flat fills only. One optional flat shadow shape at 8–12% opacity.
- Shapes should be chunky, geometric, slightly off-centre, and easy to read.
- Corners are soft, but not every object is a pill or bubble.

## Signature motif
Use one subtle recurring quirk across the family: a small off-centre notch, cut, seam, or corner bite in large shapes. It should feel designed, not decorative.

## Faces
- Eyes: simple dark oval/dot + one tiny catchlight maximum.
- Mouths: single stroke or flat mouth shape.
- Noses: minimal mark; never realistic rendering.
- Age is communicated by silhouette, hair, posture, and 1–3 deliberate marks.
- Expressions should remain readable at 48 px.

## Pets
Simplify species to silhouette + 2–3 defining traits. Coats are flat patches, not fur texture.

## Achievements / events
Use one central object. Combine two symbols only when one object cannot communicate the meaning.

## Rarity / premium
Rarity comes from the frame, not from adding glow and particles to the base art.

## Export
- PNG: RGBA, transparent.
- SVG: expand appearance; round joins preserved.
- Avoid baked drop shadows outside the safe area.
- Pixel-check every icon at 24, 32, 48, and 90 px.
