# ONE MORE LIFE — v0.8 Runtime Test Plan

This is the gate between "implemented" and "release-ready." Run it against the exact packaged v0.8 project.

## 1. Boot and migration
- Open the project in Godot 4.4+ and run it normally.
- Load at least one real v0.7 save. Confirm the life, NPCs, money, homes, conditions, Life Threads and special-life data remain intact.
- Age up twice, save, quit, reopen and load again.
- Confirm a new `ambition` state appears without wiping older state.

## 2. Automated headless smoke
Run:

```text
godot --headless --path . res://tools/v08_system_test.tscn
```

Expected result: `failures=0` and exit code 0.

## 3. Ordinary career depth
- Hold at least three unrelated normal jobs.
- In each one use Career Development: project, networking, mentor, rival, training.
- Confirm reputation/network change and persist after save/load.
- Confirm coworker NPCs created by networking/mentorship appear in People where appropriate.
- Age several years and verify a professional rival can recur.

## 4. Police / Detective
- Become a police officer and promote to Detective.
- Work at least 10 patrol calls.
- Take each case type at least once using test/save manipulation if necessary.
- Process scenes, interview witnesses and interrogate suspects.
- Win and fail the Evidence Board minigame manually.
- Attempt warrants at low and high evidence.
- Make at least one weak arrest and one strong arrest.
- Let a case age into the cold-case path and reopen it.
- Trigger at least one Internal Affairs concern.
- Save/load with an open case and confirm suspect, witness, clues and evidence remain.

## 5. Medicine / Surgery
- Enter the doctor career from Resident and advance ranks normally.
- Choose several different specialties.
- Take patients through symptoms -> diagnosis -> second opinion -> treatment.
- Win, partially complete and fail the Operating Room minigame.
- Confirm successful surgeries, complications and malpractice all produce different consequences.
- Confirm burnout moves with repeated clinical play and can interact with the existing mental-health system.
- Save/load with an active patient.

## 6. Sports Pro
- Start at least three sports.
- Complete ten seasons on one athlete.
- Inspect the standings every season and verify the player's record matches the table.
- Reach postseason/finals at least once.
- Win a league championship.
- Let a contract expire and verify free agency.
- Request a trade successfully and unsuccessfully.
- Use an agent before a contract window.
- Trigger an injury and use the second-opinion action.
- Retire and confirm the career history remains meaningful in the life record.

## 7. Pets
- Train a pet from low training to high training.
- Enter shows before and after grooming/training to verify those systems affect results.
- Use the vet after health declines.
- Breed a pet; verify offspring are separate NPCs.
- Certify a support animal.
- Open and run each pet-business type over several years.
- Continue as a child while a family pet is alive and verify the pet profile persists.

## 8. Enterprise
- Run the original hands-on business and at least three portfolio companies at the same time.
- Found a company and acquire companies with both cash and debt.
- Pull dividends and verify value/quality tradeoffs.
- Force a company into debt/bankruptcy through test values.
- Confirm CEO NPC references remain valid after save/load.
- Name a child successor, die/continue as that child and verify the portfolio transfers only when intended.

## 9. Justice / reentry
- Produce acquittal, dismissal, plea and conviction histories.
- Confirm evidence strength changes trial odds.
- Win and lose an appeal.
- Complete probation check-ins and trigger a warning while heat/record pressure is high.
- Verify reentry events can affect jobs, housing and relationships.

## 10. v0.8 events
- Exercise every v0.8 event at least once with a test harness or debug state.
- Run every choice and every reachable outcome branch.
- Confirm all 18 delayed-followup source events fire their intended callbacks.
- Verify no v0.8 event exposes impossible actions for the current profession/state.

## 11. Presentation and controls
- Play Evidence Board and Operating Room with mouse and keyboard.
- Verify patrol siren, clinical monitor, crowd, acquisition cash, gavel and championship fanfare respect Settings volume/mute.
- Test Light/Dark and every special-life theme with the new menus.
- Test 1280x720, 1600x900, 1920x1080 and one ultrawide resolution.
- Verify no new text clips at increased interface size.

## Release gate
Do not call v0.8 public-release ready until failures are fixed and the exact fixed package is re-run through the static audit + this runtime plan.
