# Completion work included in v0.82

3 October 2026. These changes follow the v0.81 development application. The owner
requested a v0.82 interim package so development can resume later. This is not
a v1.0 release. All broad acceptance gates remain in the
[completion register](COMPLETION-REGISTER.md); packaged checks are recorded in
[v0.82 validation](releases/V0.82-VALIDATION.md).

## Original Story Life replacements

The owner chose six additional original campaigns. The inspected legacy catalog
had four TV-based previews, rather than the six counted in the question. Those
four were replaced with the six additions; the six existing originals remain.

| New campaign | Character | Premise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stormpost Service | Pip Parcel | Keep a drifting-island postal route alive |
| Moon Shift | Della Quirk | Protect a lunar laundromat and its crew |
| Lantern League | Kiri Kite | Compete in a night-market relay |
| The Last Receipt | Cass Ledger | Investigate a cafe's false accounts |
| The Missing Tuesday | Nico Moss | Recover a town's traded memories |
| Detour Season | Ari Brake | Decide what survives a closing bus depot |

Each addition has ten independently written scenes and three reachable endings.
Remembered choices change routes and final resolutions. Scene numbers follow
reading order rather than character age or internal branch indices. Reading a
scene does not advance a simulation year. Genres and search reach all twelve
campaigns, with one scrolling surface and the existing compact choice cards.

Four older preview IDs migrate to the first four replacements. Their exact
journals and logs are archived under Earlier journal before a living reader
starts the new story. An explicit notice explains the change. Completed lives
remain completed, and no migration ending reward is added. Stale preview callbacks
cannot advance an original scene. Older original campaigns retain their writing
and choices while their log headings migrate to scene order.

## Study and original faces

Courses have 84 basic and 28 advanced questions across 28 employment fields. Required units
use a dedicated completion record, so optional-question exhaustion does not
strand a paid course. Saved questions remain fixed and yearly unit guards apply.
Qualifications are permanent rather than trimmed as event history.

Community college awards a separate associate qualification in supported
US/Canadian settings. Related bachelor study may credit two years; unrelated
study may credit one. A new degree always requires further study. Cross-border
credit requires an explicit credential review; professional licences remain
separate. Major changes show retained/lost years, fees and remaining study.
Interruption/return retains the transcript and debt, with the former scholarship
ending on return.

A completed practical course can now lead into college study in the same field.
Two units are credited, fees fall to 60%, and at least one further year is required.
An advanced field-specific capstone and final assessment award the separate
associate qualification. Previous skills and work samples are not awarded again.

Placements now have 56 distinct scenarios and 168 authored outcomes across the
same 28 fields. Each supervised shift records its task, choice, result, payment
and actual supervisor. A readable placement record preserves those decisions.
Two honest shifts earn field evidence once and create a later connection with
the supervisor; a reference also requires their trust. High trust alone cannot
turn unsupported work into a reference. Failed work retains a recovery route,
while successful-field evidence stays guarded when visible history is trimmed.
Older saved questions still resolve without the new scenario payload.

## School work and remembered collaborators

All ten school clubs now have two distinct authored situations and three
outcomes each: 20 situations and 60 outcomes. Plans modify challenge results,
strain and trust. Actual classmates recur across years, remember the decision,
and support a later connection. Situations are consumed once per person; an
exhausted pool retains ordinary practice instead of repeating its encounters.
The club work record shows tasks, classmates, choices and results.

School projects now have actual teachers. Pausing preserves choices, quality,
costs and yearly limits; returning costs one time. Closing preserves an unfinished
record without completion rewards. Lost collaborators can be replaced, with the
handover retained at completion. A paused final presentation may return through
age 20 after graduation. Student council projects start at age 13.

The shared person lookup now rejects empty identities. Previously it could match
an unassigned family member, attaching a connection to the wrong person.

## Estate continuity and executor duties

After a living transfer to a child, the former player's later death now settles
their own cash, investments, debts and owned objects among actual heirs. Named
heirs retain identity across viewpoint changes. Negative-equity residences are
settled against the estate rather than passed to a child with a debt shortfall.
Transferred assets leave the former owner's saved inventory; collection history
remains. Reloading an already settled death does not pay a second inheritance.

An adult executor can review claims, check the actual allocations and file a final
report. Mistakes require a later correction; reporting choices affect family
trust. Delegated or inactive executors advance one remaining step per year.
Administration never redistributes the estate or charges heirs its old debts.

Known parent/child/sibling links now give surviving NPCs their own dated loss,
bounded emotional reaction and support history. Annual background recovery,
former-player snapshots and later viewpoint changes retain those records.
Living and death handovers preserve a child's recorded stress alongside their
other stats. Unrelated people are not given invented family bereavements.

Matching original baby, child and older-person vectors now provide facial
expressions and the existing cosmetics across all six tones. Neutral faces keep
the original glyph; adult head geometry remains unchanged. The 42 added vectors
have pinned Microsoft Fluent attribution and the bundled MIT notice. The shared
texture cache stays bounded.

Loading now deep-copies mutable save state. Migration and subsequent play cannot
modify the caller's cached backup or historical snapshot.

## Remaining limits

- v0.82 is an interim development package. Final v1.0 installation, upgrade,
  packaged migration and broad acceptance checks remain.
- Special adult hair/themed looks still use the original glyph without the new
  expression extensions. Whole-world avatar variety and visual acceptance remain.
- Regional recognition is a fictional US/Canada rule, not every region or era.
  Campus histories and wider placements still need completion and acceptance.
- Existing original stories, other modes, relationships, school/work histories,
  estates and the remaining roadmap need their wider acceptance work. The new
  catalog does not establish completion of those systems.
- The GitHub releases list was rechecked and remains empty. No public release,
  licensing change or rewritten historical Git material is claimed here.
