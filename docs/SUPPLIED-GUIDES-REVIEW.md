## v0.42 implementation boundary

This pass refines 36 projects across 12 existing ordinary occupations, adds 12 new project briefs (190 total across 89 jobs), stores choice history and work rolls, forbids these refined projects from recycling in the same life, and ties audit performance penalties to the originating employer session. It does not add the missing occupations or complete the other themes. The entire accepted campaign in NEXT-UPDATES.md remains open; this review supplements it rather than replacing it.

## Combined v0.43–v0.50 review

Six ordinary occupations now address the missing career foundations above. Baker is separate from chef. Vehicles have owned condition/mileage/resale and a paid skill-gated DIY repair route; this does not make every mechanic project operate on a customer's actual vehicle. Museum management now has physical exhibits, annual demand, costs, admission, provenance disclosure and handover; venue fit-out remains sunk and object-only inheritance is used after death. Enterprise and client decisions gain variety and durable records. Longer case campaigns, actual museum authentication/theft, zoo breeding, special-career contracts and the other open themes remain unfinished.
