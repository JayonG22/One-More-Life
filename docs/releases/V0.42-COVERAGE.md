# v0.42 Work With Consequences

Open Work → Occupation → Projects. Doctor, pharmacist, vet, lawyer, mechanic,
chef, executive, professor, scientist, Army, Navy and Air Force each have three
contextual projects. There are 36 refined projects, 108 stages and 324 authored
option records. Option wording is reused where the same professional approach
makes sense; these counts do not mean 324 unique stories. Twelve briefs are new;
the full ordinary catalogue now has 190 projects across 89 occupations.

Planning, execution and reporting make different demands. Detailed checks cost
extra time. Peer review gives a better chance but smaller quality gains. A
narrower scope is safer but has a lower quality ceiling. Readiness, field skill,
mentor support and speciality affect execution. Strong results earn samples,
professional reputation and client trust that support existing promotion rules.
Success remains probabilistic. The execution roll is saved with the prompt;
reloading the same prompt does not draw a new roll.

The three choices are recorded in Work record. False completion reports lose
trust and face the existing next-year bonus recovery. Performance penalties now
require the original job and employer session. Switching employers does not erase
the financial or reputation consequence. Legacy records without a session keep
their repayment/reputation outcome but cannot penalise a current post.

Refined projects are consumed once per life, including withdrawal. Existing
completed history also prevents old-save recycling. Disabled rows explain this;
routine practice, training and mentors remain available. Other jobs still have
their preceding two-project templates and do not yet have this non-repetition rule.

Existing saved active briefs continue with their saved format. No new degree,
licence or career is automatically granted. Avatar, dark themes, shopping and
monthly infancy/yearly ageing remain as previously implemented.

The supplied guide review records every occupation row and groups the systems
and education/ribbon references. These guides are outlines with conflicting
claims, not proof of complete current BitLife coverage. Missing professions,
agency/dealer/museum management, recurring patient/client identities, actual
vehicle repair integration and longer career arcs remain future work. All prior
accepted campaign requirements remain open until verified, before v1.0.

This is a local preview. No GitHub release or remote push was made.
