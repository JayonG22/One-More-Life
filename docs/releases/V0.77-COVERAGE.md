# v0.77 — Life Has Weight

A development increment toward the full accepted game. No complete campaign,
mode, balance, player-acceptance or v1.0 gate is closed by this release.

## Remembered people and practical coping

Health & support and the existing Mental health panel now reach Life & wellbeing:
stress, grief, connection and rest. This is an emotional life course alongside the
existing diagnostic record, not a second diagnosis list or a moral rating.

Actual close-person deaths record name, identity, relationship and year, with
bounded remembered strain. A duplicate death callback cannot settle grief,
inheritance or ordinary death effects twice. The old permanent lost-person flag
is consumed; it no longer repeatedly manufactures clinical grief many years after
a loss. Existing flags with no known identity are recorded as earlier loss without
inventing a person. Recent loss and age-appropriate eligibility still influence
the established clinical grief route. Remembering eases some strain while keeping
the person and history; later anniversaries can feel different without forcing
another identical question.

Positive existing relationship activities register actual contact once per person
per year. Contact must still be with a living person to contribute to connection.
A dedicated conversation costs time, requires an available trusted real person,
changes the actual bond and leaves a memory. Therapy, groups and quiet weeks also
contribute through their existing cost/time guards. Quiet pleasure eases a little
ongoing strain; positive hobby collaboration registers the actual person instead
of granting a detached friendship point.

Gentle routines fit low health; protected rest costs more time; continuing to push
records extra strain. Plans need annual renewal. Recent routines/support influence
next-year stress, connection and rest strain. High sustained strain has explained
mood/stress effects. Burnout reduces relevant work/study/creative readiness by a
bounded amount without reducing innate intelligence or changing a person's worth.
Free coping remains available in personal debt; paid minor support uses household
resources and assistance, preserving personal gifts.

## Scenes, follow-ups and continuity

Sixteen authored personal moments have forty-eight distinct conclusions. They are
requested through the relevant topic, selected from unseen eligible scenes and
stop on exhaustion. School and work situations require actual schooling (including
university) or employment. Grief scenes name a person on the actual loss record.
Costs, strain and mood change according to the chosen option. The following year
records what came of the choice and whether support continued or needs renewal.
Identity, year and resolved-choice guards prevent stale/replayed effects.

Histories are bounded and paginated. Actual emotional packets carry through living
and inherited child transfers. Inheritance adds the actual newly lost parent
to the child’s emotional/life record while retaining earlier losses and clinical
records. Recorded former characters continue saved routines,
contact recency, strain, loss adaptation and due follow-ups off screen, with annual
settlement guards. Their returning snapshot stays synchronized. This advances
existing plans; it does not invent a complete independent NPC counselling/social
calendar or every missing NPC relationship motive.

The preceding gradual dependence recovery, clinical care, company work, original
modes, quiet-life projects and latest emoji-style avatar refinement stay included.
