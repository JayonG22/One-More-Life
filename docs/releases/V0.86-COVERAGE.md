# v0.86 — People and Lasting Stories

An interim content and continuity update, continuing the approved roadmap.
The full v0.86 campaign acceptance remains open. This package does not claim
feature completion or v1.0 readiness.

## Included

- A shared recent-scene history prioritizes unfamiliar situations across local
  save slots: 18 recent scenes and four recent families, with a bounded 64-entry
  history. Limited pools fall back to available content; infinite non-repetition
  is not promised. Saved pending prompts remain unchanged.
- Relationship-changing scenes can retain smaller important moments in Life
  Threads. Repeated moments with the same person merge, keeping the latest eight;
  the thread detail page shows earlier moments, and profiles summarize shared
  history. Later conversations affect the actual bond. Unavailable people lead
  to reflection rather than fabricated contact.
- New situations cover shared school credit, shift coverage, child activity fees,
  friendship across distance, sibling borrowing, independence from parents,
  friendship boundaries, later-life visits and shared household decisions.
- Career situations cover repair work, care handovers, lesson planning, digital
  review and kitchen allergy notes. Career filters now honor authored `job_id`
  requirements. Work choices affect performance and the named colleague.
- Authored personal effects change a named NPC's happiness or stress alongside
  relationship effects. Personal effects are bounded and do not affect deceased
  NPCs. The child-fee scene requires a child aged 6–17.
- Six new school-person situations and age/stage filters keep preschool,
  kindergarten, elementary, middle-school, high-school and college encounters
  appropriate to the current stage. Selected decisions enter the existing
  Connections system for a later named-person follow-up.
- Motives appear in optional profiles. Already-open promises show their deadline
  instead of offering another identical promise.
- First-word and first-step scene notices no longer duplicate developmental
  milestones. The childhood lost-tooth scene is single-use.

## Preserved and remaining

Existing clubs, cliques, duties, classes, NPC records, dark themes and original
avatar style remain included. This batch adds no new top-level destination.

Broader NPC networks, reconciliation branches, regional education, wider career
and activity content, adulthood integration, balance and release acceptance
remain in [NEXT-WORK](../NEXT-WORK.md) and the
[four-update campaign](../FOUR-UPDATE-CAMPAIGN.md). Finite authored content can
still run out. Existing older pools have not all been rewritten to include NPC
personal effects or unique follow-ups.

No gameplay testing was performed, at the owner's request. See the
[delivery notes](V0.86-VALIDATION.md).
