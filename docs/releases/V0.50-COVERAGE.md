## Catalogue and play

The ordinary catalogue now has 95 jobs and 202 work projects, including 48 refined three-stage projects; 130 career dilemmas and 190 knowledge tasks. These counts describe records, not wholly independent life campaigns. The six new jobs add twelve projects and twelve dilemmas to v0.42. Twelve independent enterprise commitments contain 36 stages. Eight client-review scenarios share three response categories. Authored content remains finite; ordinary practice and existing activities remain available when a fresh pool is exhausted.

Assets → Ownership & everyday use; Assets → My museum; Work → Occupation → Clients; More → Connected lives → Ambitions & enterprise. Existing dark themes, avatar design, short wrapped wording, navigation distinction, costly bulk activities, monthly infancy and yearly later ageing remain. No time slider is restored. Mature career content is age-gated, preference-gated and non-graphic.

Museum exhibits move rather than copy. Closing returns them. Fit-out is a sunk cost. Living handover transfers the museum or gifts objects if the child already has one. After death museum objects join the heirlooms; the venue is not kept open automatically. The present property inheritance allocation still needs a broader multiple-heir audit. Slightly altered fictional names do not by themselves guarantee legal clearance; this preview does not certify that.

## Added childhood household request

Assets → Family budget separates personal savings from household support. Generated childhood event money costs and emergency hospital care draw from living parent funds; any uncovered portion is recorded as household assistance, never as personal child debt. Negative childhood cash balances from older saves or incidental direct charges are settled at load/change/annual boundaries. Voluntary purchases still require affordable personal cash; support is not an unlimited shopping grant. Formal student/other loan records are not silently erased. Childhood wages and gifts stay personal unless contributed deliberately. A child aged six or more can transfer 25%, 50% or 100% of their current cash to each living parent once yearly, with actual payer/payee changes and family memory.

Support ends at 18 by default; per-life household agreements allow 16 or 21. These are fictional game agreements, not purported country laws. Independence ends coverage without taking earned cash or giving an automatic grant. A child with no savings starts at zero; savings and gifts are preserved. Poor backgrounds remain possible; their budgets and other challenges are retained while dependent children are protected from impossible automatic cash debt. Poor-birth probabilities were not changed. Exact real-world country-age rules and a household-wage pooling percentage remain open rather than inventing universal rules.
