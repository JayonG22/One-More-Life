# v0.40 Connected Lives

This local preview combines the v0.33–v0.40 work into one update. It builds on
v0.32 Work and Independence, shopping, dark themes, original avatar art, costly
bundles and the original monthly infancy / annual later-life progression.
It is not v1.0 or a claim that the full 180-item campaign is complete.

## Where to find it

Open **More → Connected lives** for eight organised categories. School and skills
also appear in Activities, commitments in a person's relationship panel, and
practical challenges in Work projects. Search includes the main destinations.
Pets and Prison expose the identity category, keeping human activities unavailable.
Original campaigns are in **Main menu → TVLife → Original**.

## Costs, limits and consequences

- A promise costs no time to make; fulfilment costs one time and the advertised
  support fee. Forty records are retained; an open promise cannot be erased by
  filling the list. Repair gains build across years rather than instant forgiveness.
- Shared / primary / weekend care uses 65% / 100% / 40% of the ordinary dependent
  support cost. Missed contact damages the bond; changing a plan takes time.
- A scored challenge costs one time plus locally adjusted $25. Only a result of
  60% or more gains the relevant skill. Relevant jobs/school/interview records use
  the result. Practice earns no stats, money, achievement or portfolio progress.
- Contracts have three paid-time stages and a two-year deadline. A cheap supplier
  cuts startup cost 25% but reduces quality. Premium pricing pays 15% more at quality
  85+, otherwise 30% less. Dishonest or abandoned deliveries lose trust. A delayed
  invoice is counted as income only when paid; finishing a closed contract pays nothing.
- School/community stages are limited to one per year. Completed relevant school
  projects add at most 6% to an application; institutions add at most another 3%.
- Move preparation costs one time and locally adjusted $250. An advertised regional
  move costs $3,000 locally adjusted and may end the job: risk is 35% when prepared,
  60% otherwise. An owned primary home is sold as part of moving. Preparation does
  not eliminate the risk or transfer a local community project to another place.
- Migration preparation costs two time and locally adjusted $300, adding 5% to
  this game's application chance for two years. Arrival does not preserve every
  licence. Reviewing credentials costs one time and $150; renew licences separately.
- Home adaptations cost two time and $600 / $2,200 / $1,200 before local adjustment.
  Selling and replacing a home clears its identity; inheritance preserves an
  adaptation only when the same physical home is inherited.
- Caregiving costs two time/$80 personally or one time/$800 for support. Phased
  work at 55+ halves wages and lowers annual stress. Mentoring and memoirs need 50+.
- Giving a possession removes it from the giver. Company handover takes two time
  and $500, transfers the portfolio and removes it from the parent. Dormant owners
  receive an annual company ledger; returning to their viewpoint does not award
  another copy. An estate dispute can share actual inherited cash, never pay a
  second bequest. Named-heir cash rules remain 70% named plus 30% shared.
- Original stories have mutually exclusive branches; one playthrough does not read
  all ten nodes. End screens show the actual chapter count and selected ending.

## Scope still open before v1.0

The [completion plan](../V1.0-PLAN.md) and [180-item campaign](../NEXT-UPDATES.md)
remain authoritative. These combined additions do not close every broad campaign
item. v0.41–v0.49 must audit and expand subject question pools, individual club/team
seasons and childhood circumstances, NPC initiative/motives, specialised businesses
and creative formats, regional institutions and migration recognition, and the
complete estate/asset allocation model. The primary home, property portfolio and
remaining possessions still pass to the selected playable heir under the existing
asset rules; an executor is recorded, not a separate fully simulated probate NPC.
School support influences learning, but wealth is not a comprehensive opportunity
simulation. The new music challenge is phrase recognition, not a replacement for
the existing live rhythm game. Longer campaign writing and wider mode interconnection
also remain part of the coverage audit.

The later balance, save migration, performance and representative player-testing
phases remain required. A dark accessible interface and focused passing checks
do not establish exhaustive compatibility or superiority to another game.
