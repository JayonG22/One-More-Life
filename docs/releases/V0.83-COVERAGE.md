# v0.83 — School & Work Communities

Development playtest package requested by the owner. Existing v0.82 features remain.

- **34 individual clubs:** the original ten plus astronomy, coding, chemistry,
  ecology, engineering, mathematics, languages, photography, film, creative
  writing, poetry, choir, orchestra, dance, crafts, cooking, gardening, animal
  care, first aid, journalism, Model UN, history, enterprise and board games.
  Up to four memberships; age requirements and distinct playable tasks. Joined
  clubs have practice controls directly in the club list.
- **18 cliques:** the original nine plus bookworms, eco kids, makers, quiet kids,
  comedians, outdoor kids, the style crowd, activists and high achievers. The
  active group has a gathering action directly in the clique list. Choices
  affect named classmates and stay remembered.
- **School stages:** preschool, kindergarten, elementary, middle, high school and
  college. Thirty-two subjects and 64 new assessment questions use stage-specific
  material. Practice and assessment are separate actions. Grades and subject
  evidence carry forward; moving stages does not reset poor performance.
- **Progress:** separate stage-progress, academic-performance and attendance
  bars. Missing class reduces attendance; attendance affects annual results.
  Yearly reports retain grades and attendance. High-school GPA begins at 14.
- **Actual school people:** a principal, nurse, three teachers and eight classmates
  per current community, with permanent identities, profiles and interactions.
  Moving stages preserves earlier people; close classmates become friends and
  other former classmates/staff remain in past relationships. Vacancies are
  filled after a death without overwriting earlier memories.
- **Named situations:** eight new school situations with 24 choices affecting the
  NPC's own mood/stress as well as relationship stats, player outcomes and
  attendance. Principal/nurse actions use real staff rather than anonymous rolls.
- **Simpler people screens:** relationship, mood and health first; **Profile**
  opens the original detailed stats, personality, personal records and memories.
  The underlying simulation remains intact. Role-bearing events show short
  summaries for up to two actual participants before the choices.
- **Work uses the same structure:** performance/skill bars, named colleagues with
  profiles, occupation-specific duties, themed encounters and existing projects.
  Every ordinary job exposes its two established task names as repeatable annual
  duties, with modest performance gains and time/stress costs. No extra salary
  is manufactured. Work choices affect the actual colleague's mood/stress and
  memories. Existing specialist careers remain; their wider depth is not closed.

The school-stage model is a simplified default sequence, not a claim to simulate
every national system. Class material is project-authored and inspired by
[grade-specific mathematics standards](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/)
and [NCES school-level definitions](https://nces.ed.gov/pubs2015/fin_acct/chapter6_8.asp).
It does not constitute a complete real-world curriculum. Authored assessment
banks are finite; after exhaustion, other subjects and class practice remain.

For later updates: more questions within individual grades, electives and majors,
teacher specialisations, term schedules, school transfers, fuller attendance
causes, personalised remedial routes, colleague/client scenarios, specialist
career duties and richer cross-stage consequences. These extend the same
people–progress–activities pattern without removing existing options.

No v1.0 completion, GitHub publication or full release acceptance is claimed.
The [remaining-work list](../NEXT-WORK.md) and
[completion register](../COMPLETION-REGISTER.md) retain the broader scope.
