# v0.29.0 — More Ways to Live

This local preview delivers the first implementation batch following the BitLife
comparison. The [ongoing register](../UPDATE-REGISTER.md) preserves all 180 campaign
items and additional comparison gaps. Later work remains open.

## Playable additions

- **Occupation → Business → Venues & ventures:** open/manage a casino, museum or
  intelligence agency. Venues have separate operating reserves, staff, maintenance,
  promotion, yearly results, recent history, withdrawals and confirmed sale.
- Casinos have three room types and a responsible-play program; staffing affects
  capacity. Gambling stakes remain separate from venue accounts.
- Museums have three curated exhibitions, admission settings, staffing effects
  and community collection evenings. This is not yet personal collectible display.
- Agencies recruit up to four operatives, train them, and take one protective or
  investigative assignment per year. Assignments are abstract fictional mechanics.
- The accepted casino route now creates the actual operating venue. Older accepted
  purchases migrate without another charge. Unaffordable operating losses can
  close a venue; earnings remain in reserve until withdrawn.
- The luxury route opens the **Velvet Society**: annual dues, yearly gatherings,
  a new remembered contact, community arts grants and leaving/lapsed membership.
  The route no longer falsely promises a house and boat or guaranteed income.
- **Occupation → Find work → Producer studio & CloudCrowd:** choose one of six
  genres, begin a project, collaborate, mix with the rhythm minigame, release to
  listeners and receive declining royalties. Confirm a one-time license sale or
  retain independent rights. Recent release history is capped at forty tracks.
- **OnlyPals:** adults can set subscription fees, publish up to three non-graphic
  releases per year, choose privacy, receive net annual income and close the channel.
  No explicit sexual scenes or images are added. Billing stops when closed.
- **More → My household & week:** see the actual last household bill breakdown,
  existing roommate rent sharing, recorded dependents, chores/service choices,
  fatigue/rest and four presets for the existing routine system.

## Function and continuity

Venue reserve withdrawals preserve net worth. Annual operations, creator payments,
agent training and certain social actions cannot settle repeatedly within one year.
Save/load preserves catalogs, operatives, reports and venues. Interrupted studio
mixing resets its active lock when loading, allowing a new attempt.

Living-child transfer retains the parent's venues in their former-player snapshot;
the child does not acquire them. Dormant former-player venues operate once per
year. On ordinary post-death succession, venue sale estimates enter the existing
estate cash calculation, rather than copying a venue and paying its value again.
Creator catalogs/club activities are not fully simulated for former players yet.

Household lines reconcile to the existing living-cost debit, including its local
modifiers. The displayed breakdown does not add a second charge. Earnings from
investments, careers, creator work and venues are still separate reports, so this
is not yet a comprehensive whole-life ledger or joint-account system.

Opening a panel now selects the action column in the large-text layout. Panel
titles wrap to avoid pushing content off-screen. Themes remain dark and the
existing minigame pause/automatic-resolution settings remain in use.
Good-deed gifts now include the fields used by the possessions screen. Older
gifts with missing icons/purchase costs render correctly and retain their notes.
Creator actions explain insufficient cash/time; capped operative training does
not charge again. Existing casino owners cannot buy a second application.

## Limits

These are first playable loops, not full BitLife pack equivalents. The update adds
no new registered minigame and no new random-event JSON scenes. Staffing and
production decisions provide new actions; remaining authored stories, catalogs,
fighter/director expansions and household sharing stay explicitly queued.
The Windows package remains a local unsigned portable preview.
