# v0.88 — Household and Family Ties

An interim household and parenting batch within the approved Whole Life with
Consequences campaign. The broader campaign is not complete.

## Included

- Shared reserve cover now requires a recorded agreement. Trust alone no longer
  authorizes spending the partner's share. Discussing the agreement costs one
  time; acceptance needs trust 55+ and resentment below 35. Either returning to
  separate cover or later relationship strain pauses that permission.
- Reserve shares retain their owners. Cover uses the active player's share
  first, then an agreed partner share. Separation does not erase owned balances;
  withdrawing returns only the active person's owned shares.
- Age-up records the negative-cash shortfall associated with recurring household
  bills. Reserve cover is capped by that record, current negative cash and
  remaining owned contributions. Loan repayments, vehicle upkeep and unrelated
  purchases are excluded from eligible bill categories. Student-loan-financed
  deficits create no second cover allowance. Loans are not erased by reserve use.
- Cover is available once yearly and retained claims reduce the recorded
  allowance. Old saves without this new ledger field must reach the next age-up
  before using the new shortfall route; withdrawing an owned share remains open.
- Reserve screens separate savings plans, contributions, cover agreements and
  history. Household screens separate bills, chores/rest, routines and people.
  Existing activity bundles, household services and routine presets remain.
- Household people open actual profiles. Parenting, the reserve and existing
  health/care schedules are linked from the household page.
- Six additional parenting situations work during agreed contact, without
  assuming the child lives at home: short calls, different household routines,
  friendship repair, personal projects, independence planning and changed goals.
  Each has three immediate results and three later outcomes.
- Parenting scene selection respects whether a scene needs the home routine.
  A child living with the other parent receives contact-safe scenes; childcare
  still cannot override custody. Profiles and pending follow-up dates appear
  beside parenting choices.
- Full parenting follow-up lists prune concluded entries first. Open callbacks
  are preserved; another callback waits for yearly review when all 32 slots are
  occupied. Existing child-development records and transfer routes remain.

## Remaining

This does not complete mortgages, regional housing, legal proceedings, business
succession, every treatment/care branch or every generational case. Existing
implementations remain, with remaining depth and acceptance recorded in
[NEXT-WORK](../NEXT-WORK.md), the [completion register](../COMPLETION-REGISTER.md)
and the [campaign](../FOUR-UPDATE-CAMPAIGN.md).

Reserve cover is a player-directed action, not an automatic partner withdrawal
or an unlimited bail-out. The household bill allowance is a bounded game
accounting rule; it does not model every individual invoice. Parenting content
remains finite. No gameplay tests were performed, at the owner's request.
