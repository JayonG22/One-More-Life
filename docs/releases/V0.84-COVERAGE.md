# v0.84 — Orderly Life Hubs

This interim update simplifies navigation around systems already in the game.
The owner asked to remove the separate **Growing up** and **Skills & competition**
destinations because they made the game hard to follow. This update removes those
front doors and places their existing content where players expect to use it.

## School

Occupation → Education opens the current school community instead of inserting
every school option into the Education screen. The school hub separates:

- **Classroom:** the current grade or campus, a named homeroom teacher and the
  classmates in that grade. Opening a person still leads to their detailed profile.
- **Classes & assessments:** subjects attached to the current stage.
- **School life:** clubs, cliques, sports, challenges and the school-day choices.
- **Lessons & projects:** existing curriculum, ongoing or paused projects and
  completed work.
- **Staff & support** and **School record:** school people and prior grades have
  their own pages.

Preschool and kindergarten have readable year labels; elementary, middle and high
school use the same grade sequence (1–12). Each new school year receives a
grade-specific classroom. Prior classmates remain friends or former classmates;
old saves with a band-level roster retain those people for the first grade after
upgrade. Existing class, assessment, project and relationship outcomes are unchanged.

## Work and practice

The long occupation list is now grouped into **Your job**, **Career & growth**,
**Pay & conditions** and **Work record**. From **Your job**, role-specific duties,
workplace situations, colleagues, career projects and practical challenges each
have a clear place.

The standalone practice list has been removed. Existing challenges are grouped by
context: trade and planning, people and interviews, service work, household
budgeting, music production and martial-arts sparring. Cost, timing, scoring,
feedback, saved rounds and outcomes are unchanged.

## More and delivery scope

More is now seven destinations: Life activities, Find & guidance, Story &
consequences, People & family, Goals & collections, World & appearance, and Lives
& saves. Its former long list is grouped under these pages without adding feature
systems.

No old save data was removed. This is a menu and information-architecture update,
not a new content expansion and not a feature-complete or v1.0 release. Remaining
roadmap work stays in [NEXT-WORK](../NEXT-WORK.md) and the
[completion register](../COMPLETION-REGISTER.md).
