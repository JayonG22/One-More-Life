# v0.71 — One More Beginning

First native Windows application milestone toward the accepted v1.0 scope.
This remains a development build: the earlier campaign/table are not complete.
[The completion register](../COMPLETION-REGISTER.md) retains unfinished work.

## Application and design

- Official Windows release executable, GUI subsystem, One More Life metadata,
  original multi-size icon and separate resource pack. Double-click the EXE;
  no CMD launcher or installed editor is required. No installer/registry changes,
  signing certificate, storefront publication or reuse licence was added.
- Original simple person/circle/leaf identity follows the existing Fieldnotes
  colours, Nunito and rounded visual language. Compact layouts keep the identity
  visible. The original avatar design is retained.
- All themes keep their dark panels; accent saturation is capped. Existing bulk,
  navigation, panel-shape and wrapping rules remain. The main-menu Story Life
  name and original-first library make the original campaigns easier to find.

## New gameplay

Fresh Start is an adult-start variant of the complete Human Life simulation,
not a separate replacement economy. Three scenarios begin at 18, 32 or 45 in
the US: First keys, Second act and A bigger small world. Each starts with a
country-adjusted cash cushion, real paid rental deposit, secondary education and
an age-adjusted family/friend cast. University qualifications and past careers
are not granted. Relatives over 100 are recorded as deceased at the opening.

Each scenario has four distinct annual choice chapters (12 scenes total), real
time/money costs, skill/stat effects and remembered relationship consequences.
Completing chapters alone does not finish the scenario. Three consecutive steady
annual reviews require actual positive recorded income, health/happiness 45+ and
the scenario's priority: a cash cushion of three months' bills (with a minimum),
a completed practical course/work-training certificate, or a living friend at
60+ closeness. Reviews run after the actual annual finance settlement. There is
no deadline or cash prize. Completion records a milestone and life continues.
Any qualifying living friend can satisfy belonging; a death does not permanently
block that route. Full earlier childhood history is not fabricated for a preset.

Two original Story Life campaigns add 24 chapter nodes and their own procedural
music: Small Hours (night-shift cafe crew) and The Borrowed House (family/estate
drama). Each has a recurring cast, choices changing paths/flags, saved journal and
three reachable alternate endings. The catalog now has six original campaigns.
Loss-theme preferences gate The Borrowed House; original and reference saves
retain their existing internal mode identifiers.

## Still open before v1.0

This is not the whole requested v0.71-to-v1.0 campaign delivered in one package.
Complete professional/client, health/care, parenting, school communities, crime
aftermath, enterprise and mode operations, full minigame/accessibility coverage,
long-life balance/migration and human testing remain in the register. These new
ideas supplement accepted work. They do not replace or waive it. No superiority
or complete parity claim is made.
