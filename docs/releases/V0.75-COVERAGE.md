# v0.75 — Care That Continues

Development checkpoint. Full v1.0 acceptance remains open.

## One remembered clinical course

GP, emergency, optician, dentist, psychiatry and therapy shortcuts reach the
existing care/medical state. A legacy annual routine no longer announces recovery
or applies a second illness penalty for a recorded condition. Event treatments
can clear acute conditions; chronic conditions retain their diagnosis and review.
Existing trusted doctor/nurse contacts retain free GP access.

Appointments consume their real time and fee once per monthly infancy/yearly
period. Saved clinic rolls prevent reloads rerolling a paid diagnosis. Referrals
retain their target and queue position; specialists charge their actual fee,
unaffordable appointments remain pending, and old referrals cannot erase unrelated
new symptoms. Second opinions correct the condition and prescription record.
Inactive conditions no longer generate prescription bills. Free public visits
remain available while an adult is in debt. Household support funds childhood
appointments and prescriptions without taking gift money or creating child debt.

## Ongoing support

Control depends on recent review, a manageable routine and prescriptions. Reviews
do not stack control repeatedly within a year. Neglected or outdated plans lose
control, while flares and uncertainty remain in the existing medical system.
Acute recovery also uses care progress. Treatment, rehab and mental support reach
their actual progress records, with period guards against repeated stat rewards.

Home adjustments reduce fatigue. School/work adjustments help readiness when
health or fatigue is limiting participation, without changing intelligence,
talents, looks or moral worth. Agreements apply to the actual course/employer/home
context and can be renewed after a change. Costs and time remain visible.

Family care offers self, shared and professional schedules. They consume real
time/money, remember the recipient/helper, provide a following-year benefit and
need renewal. A deceased or unavailable helper interrupts shared care. The older
later-life care entry reaches the same schedule, avoiding two relief rewards.

## History, scenes and generations

Care history records appointments, diagnoses, reviews, costs and follow-ups in
bounded, paginated records. Thirty-six authored practical scenes cover eighteen
physical conditions with 108 distinct conclusions. They are consumed once per
person, prefer locally unseen scenes, survive reloads and stop when exhausted.
School-age limits and actual employment requirements keep unsuitable scenes out
of childhood. Quiet, standard and often settings control optional interruptions;
requested care still reports its result. Routines, stress and control respond to
the chosen outcome rather than leaving it as a detached reading panel.

Living and inherited child transfers preserve actual medical, prescription and
care packets. Established off-screen records advance conditions, prescription
bills, injuries, mental recovery progress and basic eye/hearing/dental wear.
Their annual guards carry forward, avoiding double settlement on return. Minor
NPC prescriptions use family resources/assistance rather than the child's gift
money. This is established clinical continuity, not a complete NPC provider
simulation. Full world-clinic behavior and representative lifespan balance remain
open in the completion register.

An integration check also exposed a placement issue: two honestly supervised
shifts now produce concrete work evidence even with a wary mentor. Mentor trust
separately controls the reference benefit; completed work is not silently erased.
The preceding emoji style, business work and accepted roadmap remain included.
