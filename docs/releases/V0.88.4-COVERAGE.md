# v0.88.4 — Recorded Consequences

Includes the preceding v0.88.3 company and successor additions.

- Eight additional fictional hearing questions cover timestamps, corrections,
  missing records, shared accounts, exhibit handling, estimates, common sources
  and observation/inference. New cases prefer questions outside the last four
  completed cases, with a fallback when the available pool is too small.
- Criminal hearings record the actual selected answer and whether it helped
  preparation. Previously the journal always printed the first answer.
- Verdict, sentence and corrupt-intervention rolls are retained with the case.
  Existing unfinished cases gain these values when their hearing resumes.
  Choices and preparation still change the chance; reopening a saved hearing
  does not request a fresh random verdict. Sentence ranges and consequences stay.
- Corrected paid care/company choice requirements to use the existing money
  gate. Unaffordable options now disable correctly rather than accepting a click
  that fails during resolution. Dependent-child household funding remains.

This does not complete investigations, civil proceedings, witness continuity or
all crime aftermath. Full accepted scope stays open in the completion register.
The owner will playtest; no game launch or gameplay tests were performed.
