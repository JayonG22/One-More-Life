# v0.30 — Growing into yourself

This local update includes the six childhood/avatar/stat/royalty/relationship
requests and the subsequent gameplay-depth requests. It builds on v0.27–v0.29;
it does not complete the entire approved 180-item campaign or establish that
the game exceeds BitLife. Screens and mechanics below are implemented rather
than suggestions. Validation is recorded separately.

## Childhood and the passage of time

Human infancy advances one month per press until the second birthday. Annual
systems, bills and other people's ages advance on birthdays, rather than every
monthly press. Later ages advance yearly. TV chapters and separate animal/prison
modes retain their own progression.

Seven firsts have independently scheduled, non-colliding months: social smile,
rolling, sitting, first tooth, standing, first word and walking. Each first is
recorded once in the life story and has a one-time profile achievement. Teeth
after the first do not create another milestone. Older saves start at their
existing age without invented infancy achievements.

The timing is an approximate fictional model, informed by the
[CDC milestone resources](https://www.cdc.gov/act-early/milestones/index.html) and
[ADA baby oral-health guidance](https://www.mouthhealthy.org/life-stages/babies-and-kids/healthy-habits-babies-and-kids).
It is not a developmental assessment. Families and children develop differently.

The new life-course library has **96 authored scenes**: 92 across eleven age
bands from infancy through later life, plus four prison situations. Scenes are
drawn separately; an entire age band's library is not played at once. A band's
pool is exhausted before reusing an entry, with no immediate repeat. Finite
pools can eventually repeat; mathematical uniqueness for every run is not claimed.

## Stats and earned progress

Six readiness contexts combine relevant ability, health, happiness and stress:
education, work, creative, physical, social and technical. Readiness modifies
success probabilities, trained skill and variable performance-based rewards.
School assessments, hiring, ordinary-work performance and career activities read
those contexts. University completion can require another year or end without a
qualification when performance remains inadequate.

Contracted salary is not silently multiplied every year. Pure gambling remains
chance-based, and skill challenges still require execution. High stats improve
prospects, rather than guaranteeing success. More → What my stats affect explains
the current modifiers.

## School with things to do

Occupation → Education has **30 introductory questions** across Arithmetic,
Reading, Science, Civics and Memory. A quiz draws three questions, shuffles their
answers and explains each result. A separate school-quiz mode awards a proportional
grade; licence tests still require all answers correct. These are introductory
practice sets, not a full age- and major-specific curriculum.

Memory and Science also have playable practical exercises. A practice costs a
time point, each subject/mode is limited per year, gains diminish as mastery
grows, and one attempt grants at most four performance points. Study harder now
opens an actual quiz rather than granting eight grade points immediately.
Admission, scholarships, degree completion and job qualification requirements
give grades practical consequences.

School life → School challenges & portfolio adds:

- A clique gathering about peer pressure and inclusion, changing belonging,
  popularity, relationships and the saved school history.
- Playable contributions for all ten clubs: chess knowledge, drama audition,
  robotics control, debate, art/camera work, band rhythm, council debate,
  yearbook/camera work, science reasoning and volunteer debate. These adapt
  established challenge mechanics rather than supplying ten completely new games.
- School fixtures with a tactical choice before a clutch challenge. Track,
  swimming, volleyball, cheerleading and wrestling now have their own challenge
  labels instead of falling back to a basketball label.
- Three talent-show entries: music rhythm, acting audition and dance memory.
  One entry per year prevents switching categories to farm repeated rewards.
- Class-president campaigns: read a student-priority survey, choose a platform,
  play the debate and contest the election. The winner can chair a council meeting,
  allocate a 100-point budget and face popularity consequences for delivery.

Recorded contributions build limited starting bonuses for relevant political,
athletic, music, acting, directing and astronaut careers. At adulthood, eligible
school histories offer community-campaign or coach follow-ups. Missing a club
does not permanently exclude an adult career. The current model is a first
portfolio/campaign loop; multi-year rivals, several council votes and many
distinct clique storylines remain future content.

All recorded school contributions also build a capped transferable-practice
bonus in ordinary hiring, visible in the application's fit explanation.

## Every listed ordinary job has specific tasks

All **81 listed occupations have two authored situations: 162 tasks**. For
example, developers investigate a regression and review a code change; baristas
prepare the queue and adjust espresso; teachers plan a lesson and assess an
assignment. Tasks offer a work decision, consume time and change performance
based on the response and readiness. Each task can be completed once per year.
They do not grant instant promotions or duplicate a year's salary.

The situations, appropriate response and explanation are occupation-specific.
They share a three-response decision format. This is not a full simulation of
each profession, and profession-specific equipment, clients and long projects
remain in the broader campaign.

## Fights, sports and everyday activities

All six martial disciplines have four named moves/tactics, progressively unlocked
by belts. Supervised sparring and belt assessments use the playable fight board.
Training prepares an assessment; advancement requires full preparation and a
passing result. Move choice changes damage, stamina cost or defence, with further
style differences. Ring ratings now read the stored belt field correctly.

Pro-athlete big games have a contextual tactical decision before the clutch
challenge. Six rotating moments cover time management, a covered route, an
opening, teamwork, restart concentration and fatigue. The result combines
execution, tactical judgement, training, physical readiness and limited luck.
This deepens the existing season loop; it is not a full match engine.

Walk, gym, meditation, reading, library, yoga and video games retain configurable
duration/cost/risk choices. Their last selected lengths are saved individually.
Settings → Ask activity length each time can be turned off to repeat those saved
choices with fewer prompts. Longer sessions retain diminishing returns and risk.

## Civil claims and crime navigation

Legal → Lawsuit now offers grounds for a disputed claim: debt, contract, property
damage, a false public allegation or injury. Choose representation, then play
disclosure, cross-examination and closing argument. Supported reasoning improves
case strength; gossip, concealment or invented evidence weakens it. The verdict
remains uncertain and changes money, relationships and case history. Costs are
charged once, withdrawal retains spent costs, and a saved decision stage resumes.
These are fictional cases, not advice about real legal procedures.

Murder is directly discoverable inside Crime. It is an abstract, non-graphic
adult action, with a confirmation/walk-away choice and possible death, grief,
heat and a criminal trial. It depicts no method and grants no special reward or
immediate inheritance payout.

## Configurable borrowing

Each eligible lender has an editor for an exact whole currency amount, a decimal
percentage of its available limit (for example 12.5%) and a repayment term from
one year through that lender's maximum. The live quote displays principal,
interest, total repayment, annual payment and rate. The signed debt uses those
exact values; invalid or stale offers are rejected.

The existing economy uses whole currency units, so this does not add cents.
Interest remains the game's flat-interest model, explicitly shown in the editor,
rather than a new amortising finance system.

## Avatar and relationships

The established round emoji portrait remains intact. The look catalog grows
from 27 to **38 looks**, the background catalog from 16 to **24 dark backgrounds**,
with eight rim choices and eight charms plus None. Existing indices and ownership
remain compatible. New premium looks/charms retain the Star Shop purchase rules.

The live portrait carries a changing expression badge for illness/poor health,
strain, low mood, fatigue, good spirits, steadiness and remembrance. The selected
character face remains visible. This is a condition/expression indicator, not
animated eyes or a complete facial-animation system. The appearance editor's
Save button fits the tested 175% text setting.

Relationships now groups Parents (including steps/guardians where applicable),
Siblings (including steps), Romantic and Pets. Past Relationships has two sections:
Deceased and Past relationships. Children/parenting and Friends & contacts remain
accessible separately through More. New extended families no longer spawn;
historical extended-family records in old saves remain preserved, rather than
being destructively removed.

## Royal birth and inheritance

Eligible random human births have rare fictional royal/noble rolls in the UK
and Japan. Other random births remain commoners. The probabilities are balance
settings, not real birth statistics. Noble titles are distinct from automatic
heirs to the throne. Siblings' age/order and the configured succession rule
determine eligibility; living-child transfer retains the child's position.

The default present-day UK setting uses eldest-child succession; historical or
explicit settings support male preference. Japan uses male-only succession.
These simplified fictional house rules draw on the
[Royal Household's succession explanation](https://www.royal.uk/encyclopedia/succession?os=win)
and [Imperial Household Agency's institutional explanation](https://www.kunaicho.go.jp/en/learn/institution/seido/seido03.html).
Adult court introductions are optional, not forced child marriages. This is not
a complete simulation of every real monarchy or distant dynastic branch.

## What carries forward, and what remains

Resolved choices now have saved decision memory: their source, chosen response,
first/last age and count. Existing flags, relationships, arcs and effects continue
to operate. The new school portfolio has concrete later-career and follow-up
effects. Saved trial prompts resume; interrupted minigames end without a reward,
retaining time/costs already spent, so a reload cannot lock the activity forever.
More → Choices I remember makes that history readable in play.

Saved memory does **not** mean every historical choice already has a bespoke
future scene. Connecting more childhood decisions to adult friendships, jobs,
health, family reactions and endings remains an explicit workstream. Random
reusable events now have a default eight-year cooldown and a two-occurrence
per-life ceiling; required court/family consequences still resolve, and optional
life-course scenes respect the yearly popup budget.

Next useful depth includes multi-year school friendships/rivals, apprenticeships
with mentors, work projects that span several years, injury recovery and adapted
activities, promises with visible follow-ups, retirement communities and broader
subject/major-specific question banks. These are recommendations and queued work,
not features represented as delivered in this package.
