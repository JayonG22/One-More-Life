# v0.72 — Work That Carries On

A development checkpoint in the active effort toward the accepted v1.0 scope.
The full feature-complete gate remains open. The native Windows application,
original identity, muted themes, avatar style and six original campaigns remain.

## Connected company operations

All twelve existing industries gain two distinct product/service plans, three
named original supplier options, three pricing policies, three staff budgets and
three capacity plans. Changes use real time, are limited annually, and feed the
existing revenue/payroll/interest/profit model rather than a separate reward loop.
Quality supports premium prices; supply affects cost/quality; morale changes
demand; stretching staff and investing in them have opposing costs/consequences.
Region, economy and the established career synergy still affect active companies.

24 authored industry issues have 72 choices with concrete costs, morale, loyalty,
quality and narrative aftermath. Each scene is consumed once per person, with
shared novelty preference between lives; exhausted pools do not repeat.
Named customers have two saved work stages, deadlines, stored outcome rolls,
quality/stat/morale-dependent delivery, remedial bills and loyalty. Reliable
customers can return. Their payments are included in annual sales, never awarded
a second time as completion cash. Product switching cannot erase a signed order.

Annual reports reconcile revenue less operating costs, payroll and interest to
profit and show the owner's actual drawing. Already-paid drawings and losses
join the household ledger without duplicate cash effects. Reopening a saved
year cannot settle the company a second time.

## A company survives its founder

The actual operating business can be handed to a living adult child or inherited
by one eligible adult. Successors may be explicitly chosen using stable person
identity. The whole company retains its name/identity, product plan, staff,
operating debt, orders, ownership stake and history. Named staff survive NPC
identifier changes. Cash allocation excludes the retained company equity;
siblings receive the recorded allocation rather than duplicate companies.
Estate debt/tax/insolvency settlement can require selling the company. Minor-only
or charity estates liquidate it; a child already owning a company keeps theirs.

Living off-screen owners use the shared company-account calculation once per
year; overdue orders, profits/losses, quality, morale and bankruptcy continue.
Returning to that child's actual life restores their existing company.

## Clearer screens and remaining depth

The Business panel links to a calm overview with separate products, suppliers,
pricing, staff, capacity, records and family pages; distinct category icons,
navigation styling, wrapping and existing Back behaviour remain. Normal and
enlarged text render checks cover all eight pages.

This pass does not close all enterprise requirements. One active product plan
is not a full per-product stock system. Inventory, research, supplier lead times,
facilities, partnership governance, restructuring, detailed personnel disputes,
longer customer histories and wider off-screen estate/world integration remain
in the completion register alongside the rest of the accepted campaign.
No scope reduction or v1.0 completion is claimed.
