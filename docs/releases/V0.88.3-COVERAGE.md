# v0.88.3 — People Behind the Business

Further partial progress on the adult-life campaign; no whole milestone closes.

- Company people links actual hired contacts and their profiles. Quality/service
  coaching costs one time and a scaled 180, one plan per colleague per year.
  There is no immediate profit or skill award.
- Saved next-year reviews use staff health/stress, company morale and pay policy.
  A successful plan adds one bounded personal skill point and one company quality
  or loyalty point. Departed/deceased staff create a conclusion without a reward.
  Pending plans and history travel with the company through handovers; background
  ownership settles the same plans without paying the active player.
- Adult children can study three company responsibilities: cash/payroll,
  customer promises and stock/supply. Each costs one time and a scaled 150;
  one lesson per child/company/year. Answers are shuffled and saved with the
  original trainee and company. Smarts, stress and owner Business skill affect
  how readily a correct answer carries into practice.
- Progress records lesson history and readiness. Further paid revision can
  improve readiness after the three lessons, avoiding a dead end after mistakes.
  Personal respect and memories reflect the shared preparation.
- A living ownership handover with three lessons and readiness 60+ adds four
  company morale; an unprepared handover reduces morale by four. This affects
  subsequent operating results. Training never becomes a compulsory transfer gate.
- Existing ownership, estate bills, stock, supply commitments, debt and customer
  orders stay with the established systems. Naming an heir remains a death plan;
  practice does not give ownership, and living transfers remain gifts.
- Pending staff plans are capped at 24 and trainee records at 32 per company;
  existing records are preserved. Development history keeps 32 conclusions and
  each trainee keeps the latest 12 lesson records. No new cash prize is added.
