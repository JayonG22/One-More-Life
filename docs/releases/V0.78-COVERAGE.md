# v0.78 — Making Ends Meet

This development increment connects personal debt recovery and keeps the accepted
scope intact. Feature complete, broad milestone acceptance and v1.0 remain open.

## Personal loans and recovery

Payment support is reachable from Money & recovery and Borrow money. Choose a
lender first: a review requires an independent adult with unemployment, insufficient
cash or poor health, one time, and adds a 2% fee (minimum $1) to the outstanding
balance. Each loan allows one arrangement. The bank and credit union offer a
one-birthday pause, two birthdays of half payments, or a longer repayment term.
The online lender offers half payments and a longer term. The informal lender
offers no hardship arrangement.

Temporary plans name their first and final payment years; original payments resume
with a recorded follow-up. A pause creates no default charge even when cash is
negative, and preserves recorded arrears. Restructuring adds three years to the
remaining term and recalculates payments over the actual balance plus fee. The
final payment cannot exceed that balance; nothing is forgiven.

Loan identities use a saved increasing serial. Clearing and reborrowing the same
amount in one year cannot reuse the identity. The bounded, paginated record keeps
agreements, fees, payments, arrears and balances. Annual callbacks cannot settle
the same personal loan, household bills or savings buffer twice.

Scheduled repayments appear in the current annual ledger. Early repayments are
reported without charging their cash again. Mortgage and education-loan payments
are excluded from living-cost scaling, so principal reduction matches the actual
charge across difficulties and price changes.

Living child transfers and inheritance restore the child's recorded loans,
credit, serial and history. Former players retain their own debts and arrangements;
their offscreen budget services payments or records default once each year.
Their obligations do not debit the character currently being played.
