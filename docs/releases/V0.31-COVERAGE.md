# v0.31 — Clearer Everyday Play

This local Windows preview implements the accepted v0.31 usability milestone and
the replacement bulk-action and lifestyle requests. It builds on v0.30; it is not the v1.0 finish line.
See [the complete-base-game plan](../V1.0-PLAN.md) and
[the remaining scope](../UPDATE-REGISTER.md).

## Original age progression retained

At the user's explicit revision on 2 October, the configurable time jump was
removed. Human infancy advances one month per click until age two; afterward,
each click advances one year. The existing infant allowance and annual time
budget remain. Pet/prison/guard modes retain their annual progression, and TVLife
retains chapter progression. There is no time-jump slider or fractional adult age.

Queued and displayed decisions survive saves. A displayed decision returns before
advancement on reload. Resolved result notices need not replay; their effects and
history persist. Saved Depth prompts restore once, rather than duplicating a trial
stage. Older saves without these fields continue to load.

## Contextual bulk activities

Eight paid bundles appear in their respective sections and in search:

| Section | Bundle | Included actions | Time | Base total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mind & Body | Full fitness session | Gym, yoga, walk | 3 | 200 |
| Mind & Body | Walk & unwind | Walk, meditation | 2 | 100 |
| Entertainment | Movie & game night | Movie, video games | 2 | 95 |
| Education | Guided study session | Read, meditate, playable Arithmetic quiz | 3 | 150 |
| Education activities | Reading & library day | Read, library | 2 | 100 |
| Health & Looks | Therapy & salon afternoon | Therapy, spa | 2 | 380 |
| Current occupation | Prepare, focus & work harder | Read, meditate, Work harder | 3 | 250 |
| Household | Household reset & proper rest | Complete chores, rest | 3 | 150 |

The displayed price applies local cost modifiers and includes an additional
convenience fee. Full combined time is charged. Preflight checks cash, time,
age, era/world restrictions, custody, enrolment, current challenges and relevant
annual limits before charging. Each bundle is available once per year. Failure
does not charge the player. Bundles preserve individual duration preferences.

Existing actions actually run, including their random injuries, overexertion,
repeat penalties and counters. The work bundle respects the existing Work harder
flag and does not resolve either job-specific task for the player. Household
actions mark chores and rest complete, so they cannot be done twice or billed as
uncompleted chores at the next birthday. The study bundle grants no quiz grades
before answering; the normal three-question quiz earns its own measured result.
These are eight specific bundles, not automatic completion of every activity or
a new unattended gameplay mode. The existing group relationship action remains.

## Lifestyle changes over time

All five main stats receive a bounded lifestyle adjustment: monthly in infancy,
yearly from age three onward. Both infant birthdays are already covered by their
monthly pass, so the extra annual lifestyle adjustment is skipped there.
Actual completed activities and successful routines count; browsing does not.
Movement, learning, relaxation, enjoyment and grooming influence the annual
result alongside stress, illness, housing, age and the existing long-term sleep,
diet, fitness and alcohol accounts. Infants use living-parent closeness as a
care proxy, plus illness and housing. This does not add a new playable infant
care activity or simulate every caregiver's daily behaviour.

Stats move partway toward the lifestyle's target rather than losing a fixed
amount regardless of circumstances. Annual lifestyle adjustments are capped at
health −10/+6, smarts −5/+4, looks −6/+4, happiness −12/+8 and stress −10/+12.
Monthly adjustments are one twelfth of the equivalent bounded annual change.
Other existing activity, illness, event, diet and body effects still apply;
these caps describe this adjustment, not a cap on every change in a year.
The formulas are game balance rather than medical predictions. High stats need
maintenance; good habits improve the trajectory, without guaranteeing success.

The actual deltas and their explanation appear in Consequences & changes.
Annual changes also enter the life log. Period guards and saved state prevent
replaying a month/year adjustment after reload. Recent activity counts reset
after the annual pass; long-term body accounts remain intact.

## Find things and return comfortably

Find & favourites is available from the panel toolbar, Activities and More.
Human search indexes main tabs, the existing activity catalog and selected
important destinations, including education, school challenges, housing, work,
contacts, children, readiness and consequences. Separate modes search their own
main tabs and settings. This is a destination/activity index, not a search of
every nested NPC, inventory item or individual minigame.

Search matches all entered words across names, categories and descriptions.
Results show their route and current age/era/enrollment/custody restrictions.
They open a destination/category without performing an action. Stars save
shortcuts across sessions; unavailable activities can be pinned for later.
Broad searches show up to 35 results and ask the player to narrow the query.
Empty results have an explanation. School shortcuts cannot bypass enrollment,
and major adult destinations retain age requirements.

Back returns to the previous panel and restores its scrolling position. Home
returns to the current tab's root. The panel trail shows the visited section
names. Keyboard typing stays in the search field rather than triggering ageing
or stealing focus. The title screen retains its grouped modes/challenges/rewards/
preferences, adds saved-life context and provides a short How to play introduction.
All existing dark themes and muted, borderless styling remain supported.

## Understand changes and consequences

More → Consequences & changes displays upcoming scheduled follow-ups and a
measured ledger of resolved decisions, actions routed through the standard action
wrapper, hosted minigame results, and monthly/yearly lifestyle adjustments. Records include cash, annual time, wellbeing,
existing bond dimensions/closeness, school/job performance and occupation changes
where those values actually changed. It does not guess guaranteed rewards from
button text. Outcome messages remain the primary explanation; some custom routes
outside these wrappers still use their own messages/logs.

The ledger keeps 80 recent records and displays the latest 30. The independent
Choices I remember history remains available. Earlier saves begin new ledger
recording from this update rather than fabricating past numerical changes.
Existing bonds without a pre-action detailed record cannot retrospectively show
their first dimensional delta; headline changes can still be recorded.

Eight existing school/teen scenes gain **16 distinct choice-linked follow-up
branches**. Group projects, pocket-money planning, science fair, exam week,
rumours, team setbacks, future planning and supporting a pressured friend each
have a different later situation for either earlier choice. Two years later,
at most one new remembered follow-up is offered per birthday; others remain
queued. The new scene quotes the exact earlier choice and lets the player use
that lesson or choose another approach. Follow-ups alter relevant wellbeing,
ability or karma; they do not rewrite a completed transcript. Each source gets
one callback per life, and reload cannot reroll the saved branch.

This connects selected choices, not every historical choice. The wider promises,
deadlines, multi-year rivals and bespoke callbacks remain future milestones.
Upcoming entries are scheduled possibilities: older follow-ups can depend on
people remaining alive and other eligibility, rather than guaranteed outcomes.

## Clearer guidance and summaries

The ? toolbar button and More → How to play explain original age progression, paid bundles,
lifestyle maintenance, navigation, readiness and lasting effects. Separate modes
get their own introductions. What my stats affect now shows the actual ability
weights, the contribution of low stress, and chance adjustments in percentage
points; it distinguishes training/qualifications from guaranteed success.

This year in my life groups recent entries into Overview, Money, People,
Work & school, Health and Other moments, then lists Upcoming consequences.
Its topic classification follows the existing log filters; it is not a complete
double-entry financial report. Up to 40 recent log entries are considered.

## Verification and remaining scope

See [validation](V0.31-VALIDATION.md) for actual results. Focused checks exercise
original monthly-to-annual progression, firsts, bundle costs and limits,
failed preflight, household/work duties, lifestyle sensitivity and period guards,
save interruptions, both branches of every new callback, measured effects and
bounded histories. UI checks cover search/favourites/routes, contextual bundles,
the real bundled school quiz, Back/scroll, help/recaps and normal/175% text layouts. Screenshots
were visually inspected. Existing mode, migration, content, minigame and long-life
checks remain release gates.

v0.31 does not claim a bespoke consequence for every choice, an exhaustive
search index, full monthly economics, complete balance, or completion of the
approved campaign. Profession projects, promises, broader school curricula,
additional modes/content and later-life/world depth stay in the v1.0 register.
