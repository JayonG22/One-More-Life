# v0.76 — Recovery That Remembers

A development increment toward the complete accepted game. V1.0 and the broad
campaign gates remain open; the release number does not establish completion.

## Habits, support and setbacks

All six existing habits use the same authoritative habit record. Community,
outpatient and residential intakes cost real money and time, preserve earlier
sessions and do not instantly cure dependence. Sessions respond to health,
happiness, stress, willpower, difficulty and an available support contact.
Programmes can change in a later year; sustained recovery can move to cheaper
self-led aftercare without deleting history.

Recovery needs pressure below 45, stress below 70 and no recent exposure for two
stable years. Support and passive improvement can move pressure down gradually.
Positive exposure restarts stability; renewed dependence preserves programme,
session and milestone records. The first recovery counts toward the established
achievement; repeated setbacks and recoveries do not farm that counter. Existing
recovered save records are adopted as earlier milestones.

Twenty-four authored pressure scenes cover gambling, shopping, overwork, drinking,
drug use and partying, with 72 distinct conclusions. Pause success responds to
readiness; giving in applies the recorded spending/health/strain effects. Overwork
also strains the actual family bond. A paid support check-in uses time and money
and keeps the boundary. The result text follows the actual outcome, including a
failed pause or an unavailable support call. Arbitrary annual DUI/possession
charges from merely having a habit are removed; actual crime actions still use
existing law and investigation systems.

Scenes are consumed once per person, survive reloads and stop when exhausted.
Quiet uses the saved boundary without popups; standard allows at most two pressure
scenes per year, often three. The same plan handles exhaustion or the scene limit.
If a saved support plan cannot be funded or lacks time, it falls back to a pause
and explains what happened. The annual settlement and choice packets are guarded
against duplicates, earlier years and another person's identity.

## People and continuity

A real available adult family member or friend can agree to be a named contact.
Death, custody or loss of closeness removes their readiness contribution. The
agreement is remembered by the actual person. Therapy and groups contribute
without repeated same-year pressure rewards; earlier healthcare entries reach
Recovery & support rather than promising a one-click cure.

Minor appointments/check-ins use household resources and assistance, preserving
personal gifts. Annual habit spending is capped at a supported child's available
cash instead of creating personal debt. Actual habits, plans and histories carry
through living and inherited child transfers. Recorded former characters continue
saved boundaries, support payments, stability, setbacks and wear off screen, with
annual guards preserved on return. Supported minor NPC check-ins use their known
parents' resources and record any assistance. This is a bounded continuation of
saved plans, not a complete independent NPC provider/counselling simulation.

The latest avatar refinement is included: larger eyes, rounder faces, soft shading
and clearer smiles within the established emoji-like style. Expressions remain
drawn into the face; saved cosmetics and actual child appearances stay intact.

## Remaining acceptance

C086–C090, C108 and G048 remain open. Wider grief/loneliness/burnout stories,
provider and community routes, whole-life/difficulty balance, all old clinical and
mode paths, adult-content preferences across the whole catalogue and broader
relationship/world integration still need acceptance. These focused mechanics do
not close unrelated campaign requirements or the full v1.0 objective.
