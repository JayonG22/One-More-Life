# v0.89 — One More Life, Unified

**Status: incomplete; carried into v0.90.** This is the single coverage record
for the large v0.89 workstream. Its open scope remains on the
[prior-update closeout board](../FEATURE-CLOSEOUT-BOARD.md). Event-memory
continuity, friction-aware relationship changes and measured age-specific yearly
recaps are integrated in the working tree. Tracked
action recaps include changes to named people's happiness, stress, health, and
smarts alongside player stats and relationship bonds. This covers authored
event choices and individual relationship actions. Recent effect entries link
to involved NPC profiles when those people remain in the save. The earlier hearing-record
and affordability work remains in the cumulative source. Routine relationship
actions and new family scenes now follow the simpler close-family model; event
roles no longer select aunts or uncles, and the royal succession rival is a
named rival rather than a newly generated cousin. Search & Favourites routes
school challenges through the School menu instead of duplicating it as another
destination; the education shortcut now has a label matching its summary page.
Legacy records remain readable. The journey-module registry is now laid out by
responsibility, with a note distinguishing current classrooms, longer school
projects and saved minigame checkpoints; retired menu names do not require
deleting these active backend systems. Work navigation now separates daily
duties, career/pay and workplace choices; career-story actions are nested under
Career & growth, role-specific police/clinical systems remain reachable there,
general justice history stays in Legal, and the duplicate workplace Training
link is removed. Raise and reference labels describe their real conditions
instead of promising an outcome. The workplace menu only offers as many
unseen scenes as remain, up to its two yearly choices, and explains when a
role's pool has been exhausted. Colleague lunches now show their regionalized
cash cost and are unavailable until the player can afford them, preventing an
unintentional overdraft. Union dues now settle with annual income and bills,
instead of being removed from cash before that year's salary is added.

Personal savings now has one control path: work's Money & recovery screen and
the planning hub both lead to the same surplus-rate and cash-floor settings.
Old saves with the former workplace buffer enabled migrate that choice into
the unified plan once. Contributions happen after regular bills and scheduled
personal-loan payments, and the household ledger lists them as account
transfers instead of expenses. Shared-reserve standing contributions now settle
after the active household's annual finances, and after passive business income
for inactive family records. The budget view shows these internal transfers
separately, alongside named income sources, annual expense lines, current debt
balances, cash, savings and net worth. Signed transfer entries also cover manual
personal-savings and shared-reserve deposits, withdrawals and bill cover.
Negative booked income is classified as a paid shortfall instead of appearing
as a negative income source; rental income after upkeep is included among the
named sources. Union dues remain a percentage-based charge and no longer receive
local living-cost multipliers.
Shopping now checks and displays a replacement-car purchase against the actual
resale credit, and home listings show income, credit and down-payment blockers
before the player opens a denied purchase.

The remaining v0.89 work is substantial: reconcile open completion-register
items; unify navigation, panels, costs and eligibility across modes; finish
display, accessibility, sound and original-avatar coverage; audit saves and
migration, balance, large families and performance; close content/mode gaps;
and make credits, documentation and the application package match the final
game.

Earlier v0.89 work notes describe implementation slices only. They are not
separate roadmap releases or acceptance milestones. At the time this coverage
note was written, the next package was planned for v1.0. The owner has since
approved substantial v0.90–v0.99 local review packages; the final consolidated
application release remains v1.0 after the owner-led playthrough and release gates.

No gameplay testing or simulation was performed during development; the owner
reserved those for personal acceptance. The update remains incomplete, and no
feature-complete or release claim is made.
