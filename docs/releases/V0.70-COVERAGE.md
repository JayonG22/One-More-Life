# v0.70 — Continuing Lives

Combined v0.61–v0.70 working preview. The full accepted scope remains in
[one completion register](../COMPLETION-REGISTER.md). This release advances
unfinished content alongside reliability/performance. Feature complete is still
not achieved; none of the missing accepted work is waived.

## Delivered behavior

- School/amateur seasons: selection/readiness, three saved tactical fixtures,
  distinct opponent approaches, stamina/practice, teammate memory and school
  evidence. Six belt-gated martial approaches improve matching counter stamina.
- Community, part-time and distance practical courses across the existing 28
  fields: three annual units, paused progress, duration, paid fees, need-based
  student fee reduction, readiness assessment and work samples. Supervised
  placements have a named mentor, two paid shifts, deadline and earned reference.
  Courses are practical evidence; they do not grant regulated degrees/licences.
- Relationships: four saved motives, once-year support, limited favours, gradual
  rival repair, six nonrecycled rivalry situations and varied invitations. Dead
  participants close requests. Trust now exists for hostile relationships too.
- Cooking, gardening, photography, crafts and reading: five three-stage projects
  with saved participants, choices, quality, earned skills and actual endings.
  Casual enjoyment remains optional, cheap and restorative.
- Eleven modes each receive three distinct authored choice chapters (33 scenes),
  remembered choices and existing mood/conduct/respect consequences. They are
  chapter expansions, not completion of every species/power/setting system.
- Hearings choose three distinct questions from 20 and preserve questions/order
  across reload. Conviction no longer counts an already recorded crime twice.
- Voluntary household reserves: separately owned player/partner contributions,
  trust/cash-limited partner participation, actual shortfall coverage, withdrawal
  of one's own balance after separation and estate release of only one's share.
  There is no interest or newly created money, and no automatic wage pooling.
- Estate execution credits every eligible child, allocates each physical object
  once, respects named portfolio successors, settles obligations before gifts,
  records tax/fees/charity, and preserves heirloom history. Equal/named/charity
  wills have receipts. Cash compensates whole-object allocation where possible;
  exact proportions can differ when cash is insufficient. Country taxes are
  fictional simulation rules. Main businesses/zoos/venues are valued for cash;
  this is not full operational succession for those businesses.
- Rental deposits are charged before being refundable. Old uncharged nominal
  deposits do not generate refunds. Rent negotiation has an annual guard.
  Free zero-cost activities remain usable when cash is negative.
- Nine workshop challenges preserve scored-round progress, answer order, disabled
  answers and Continue on reload. Ownership/token guards prevent duplicate
  checkpoint awards. This does not add mid-round resume to every original game.
- Long module menus create at most 48 action rows/20 information rows per page;
  later entries remain accessible without stacking new Back screens. Menu data
  generation is still complete; this is bounded rendering, not full virtualization.
- Dormant seasons/placements close expired commitments without invented rewards.

## Known unfinished scope

The register retains all 180 campaign items, earlier specific requests and guide
follow-ups. Major open work includes complete professional/client histories,
school communities and all childhood consequences, health/care/parenting depth,
long investigations and re-entry, suppliers/staffing and special-career contracts,
royal succession conflict, zoo breeding, museum authentication, regional/cultural
and migration histories, mode operations, all-original-minigame accessibility,
whole-life balance and wider save/multigeneration/player testing. No claim of
BitLife parity or superiority is made.

Learning consumes finite original practical questions without recycling; an
exhausted field can require another field/existing training route. The new goals,
projects, sports and mode chapters are finite foundations, not unlimited content.
Existing valid saves remain supported; back up important saves before upgrading.
No GitHub release has been published by this local packaging work.
