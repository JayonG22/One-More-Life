# v0.88.2 — Care and Recovery

This continues the adult-life campaign. It does not close v0.88 or the feature-complete gate.

- Eight care situations about classroom access, school-team expectations,
  shifts, family outings, privacy, changing plans, community participation and
  flexible backup. Each has three choices and distinct later success/setback text.
- Situations require the relevant existing condition and age/context. School
  scenes need enrolment; work scenes need a job. Existing once-per-life novelty
  and care-story frequency controls remain. This adds to existing care content.
- Care prompts retain the original person, decision, roll and work/school context.
  Chosen routines affect condition control and stress. Actual contacts gain
  memories; trust and their stress can change with practical decisions.
- Next-year outcomes use the saved roll, current control, routine continuity
  and stress. Modest control, health and stress changes do not guarantee a cure.
  Positive work/school effects require the original job session or school stage/
  course. Departed contacts receive no new relationship effects.
- A recovered condition closes its review without recreating the illness.
  Pending care reviews also conclude while a former player continues off-screen;
  background updates affect that person's care record, never the active player's
  work or grades. These background callbacks do not create new social awards.
- Care follow-ups has its own short page. Open callbacks are preserved: at 24
  pending reviews, further callback scenes wait. Closed records are pruned first
  above the 40-record limit; existing medical-history bounds remain.
- Family caregiving lists people, with one personal page per relative. Health,
  current care, helper and profile are visible together. Personal/shared/paid care
  and respite keep their previous individual choices, time and costs.
- Condition routines show a control bar and clinical review due year. Existing
  appointments, prescriptions, referrals and clinical treatments remain intact.
- Practical care costs continue through household support for dependent children;
  adult choices check affordability before charging. Original avatar art remains.
- Corrected an older club-care choice whose label contradicted its consequence.
