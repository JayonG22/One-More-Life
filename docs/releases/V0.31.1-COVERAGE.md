# v0.31.1 — Clear buttons and better shopping

This local preview adds the shopping and wording requests made after v0.31.
It includes v0.31 and does not complete the separate v0.32 career milestone.

- **Three button roles:** ordinary actions, navigation marked OPEN, and bundles
  marked BUNDLE with a subdued accent and distinct corners. Six older immediate
  actions now correctly use the action style. The ten selectable themes stay dark.
- **Shorter text:** bundle names, requirements, shopping instructions and item
  summaries are simpler. Retail purchases use “Buy · price”. Menu row titles and
  descriptions wrap; their buttons grow with the text. Popup menu options wrap too.
  This covers shared menu rows; it does not rewrite every story or remove intentional
  truncation from every compact avatar/status widget.
- **Shopping categories:** electronics, fashion, sport, music, books, jewellery,
  oddities, collectibles, transport, homes and animals. Both shopping entry points
  open this same hub. Navigation search includes shopping, home buying and vehicles.
- **Eight specialist retailers:** Best Byte, Samswoon Studio, Guccish Gallery,
  Prance & Co., Decathlawn, Sportif-ish, String Theory Music and Barns & Novels.
  They offer 44 named products. Their ranges differ: Best Byte stocks consoles and
  streaming kits; Samswoon carries professional cameras, VR and drones. Other stores
  have playful names as well. These are fictional aliases, not licensed endorsements
  or an assurance against intellectual-property claims.
- **Functional products:** existing tags, uses, requirements, effects, country
  pricing, depreciation and resale remain connected. Names and brands survive saves.
  Age and restricted-item checks run again when purchasing, not only when rendering.
- **Seven primary homes:** studio, terrace, bungalow, detached family home, villa,
  mansion and estate. Each has a different price and vector silhouette. The selected
  model, value and mortgage persist. Existing down-payment, income and credit rules
  apply. One primary home at a time; the existing financing model is retained.
- **Eleven cars:** the three existing tiers plus compact, hybrid, electric, wagon,
  SUV, pickup, roadster and limousine models. Each has its own price, annual upkeep
  and happiness effect. Electric, wagon, SUV and pickup get new drawn silhouettes.
  Licence checks, replacement trade-ins and resale remain functional.
- **Buying versus owning:** buy or rent a primary home and buy cars through Shopping.
  Assets manages the current home, current car, money, investments, investment rentals
  and possessions. Existing motorcycles, boats and aircraft are under Shopping's
  transport category. Collectible purchases also enter the same shopping hub.
- **37 animal breeds/varieties across 12 kinds:** choose a named variety before buying
  or adopting. Breed affects purchase and yearly feeding costs; shelter fees stay
  equal within a species. Personality stays individual. Breeds appear in pet details
  and save correctly. Older pets receive a stable default; new event pets receive a
  variety. Offspring receive a mixed/default variety rather than an invented pedigree.
  Acquisition rechecks age, actual price and six living-pet capacity; deceased pets
  no longer permanently occupy a slot.

Named models are catalogue variants. This update does not add separate fuel,
charging, room-by-room housing, breed-specific illness or bespoke art for every
animal variety. Those broader systems remain governed by the completion roadmap.
