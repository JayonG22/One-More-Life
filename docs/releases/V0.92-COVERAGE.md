# v0.92 — People & Relationships

**Status: in progress; no v0.92 package has been exported.** The v0.90 and v0.91
archives remain the latest interim owner-review packages. No release-candidate
or v1.0 acceptance is claimed.

## Implemented in the current source

- Background promises now distinguish a missed commitment from one that ended
  because its named recipient died or was no longer available. The latter closes
  without lowering reliability or trust; both outcomes are recorded in the
  background character's journal and history.
- Open promise rows now show whether the person is available, the deadline, and
  the time and money needed. Closed outcomes are summarized in the same panel,
  while older resolved rows stay in the journal.
- A stale promise closes with a visible explanation. An overdue promise applies
  the missed-commitment consequences rather than silently doing nothing.
- A relationship invitation records the friend's name. If the person becomes
  unavailable before resolution, the choice closes without an invented
  relationship effect. “Make time” requires one available time point, so a player
  cannot choose it without the resource; a stale choice still resolves honestly.
- An unavailable person in a delayed relationship moment now receives a clear
  no-effect conclusion instead of silently discarding the decision. This also
  applies to one-year moments and Connections follow-ups, including a life
  continuing in the background. A callback owed to the now-active living child
  resolves to that person and is written into the current journal; it is not
  mistaken for a missing contact or used to award a new life an old bond reward.
- Eight new shared-moment scenes cover later childhood, adolescence, first
  independence, work, caregiving and later-life friendship. Existing and new
  shared moments update both people’s matching stats; a former playable person’s
  saved stats stay in sync. The next-year bond reward now depends on whether the
  choice fit that person’s current priority, while the memory still resolves.
- Commitments now have a focused “Promises & plans” page, separate from the
  People screen’s relationship directory. It shows active commitments and a
  concise set of recent outcomes; individual profiles remain the route to
  person-specific actions. Pending records resolve names by persistent person
  identity, so a renamed contact does not leave stale labels in the panels.
- Child care plans show the current arrangement, support share, last review and
  visits. The panel disables a repeat plan change or visit after its yearly use,
  and makes the visit option unavailable until a plan exists.
- Person-specific actions surface the reason they are unavailable, such as an
  action already used this year, missing time or an age requirement. Repair is
  disabled when trust is already steady, and exhausted rivalry scenes stay out
  of the active choices.

## Still open in v0.92

The wider scope remains open: complete people and relationship records across
live child transfers, romance and boundaries, co-parenting, reconciliation,
past/deceased histories and follow-ups when names, roles or circumstances change.
This source slice does not close the people-and-relationships campaign or the
prior-update closeout board.

No game launch, gameplay test or simulation was performed. The owner retains
gameplay acceptance. The source will be packaged only after the v0.92 checkpoint
has enough completed scope to present as a useful review build.
