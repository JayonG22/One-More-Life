# v0.85 — Clearer Life Navigation

This interim update continues the organization pass begun in v0.84. It keeps
the existing six main tabs and all established gameplay systems, while making
the busiest entry pages shorter.

## People

Relationships now opens six counted sections: Parents & siblings, Partners,
Pets, Children, Friends & contacts, and Past relationships. Each group opens
the existing people list and profile actions. Children still support viewpoint
transfer when eligible; past and deceased people remain in the archive.

## Assets

The financial summary stays at the top. Five entry pages group related systems:

- **Money & planning:** forecasts, household contributions, savings,
  investments and loans.
- **Home & location:** owned home, home upkeep, tenancy, local life and the home
  market.
- **Transport:** owned vehicles, upkeep, commuting and vehicle stores.
- **Things & collections:** possessions, shopping, museum and zoo.
- **Businesses & property:** rentals, companies and the billionaire endgame.

**Activities → Shopping** now begins with three short groups: everyday stores,
homes & transport, and animals & adoption. Owned-home and vehicle pages link
directly to their relevant store.

## Work and everyday wording

Regular job openings are separated from **Careers & business**, which contains
career growth, companies, special careers and creator work. More menu labels and
the quick guide use shorter names and descriptions. The guide groups its advice
under time, outcomes and navigation rather than one long paragraph.

Settings opens short pages for stories, sound, gameplay, display and comfort.
The full settings popup remains available with its existing controls.

School, career, relationship, financial and life-mode mechanics are unchanged.
The update changes how existing features are reached; it does not remove or
close any accepted scope.
