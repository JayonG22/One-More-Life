# v0.28.0 — People Have Lives

This is the first implemented milestone of the approved major expansion.
The remainder of the [180-item campaign](../NEXT-UPDATES.md) is still planned.
The earlier requests remain covered by [v0.27](V0.27-COVERAGE.md).

## Added content

14 authored arcs, 56 scenes, 168 choices and 336 outcome variants. Each initial
decision has three distinct delayed branches; those branch scenes each offer
three responses. These counts describe authored definitions, not 336 unrelated
storylines. New scenes enter the usual context-aware event rotation.

| Arc | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| The date on the fridge | Showing up for a school-age child |
| A first place of their own | Adult independence and realistic planning |
| The tasks nobody wrote down | Elder care and autonomy |
| The family comparison | Sibling expectations |
| A friendship left on read | Reconnection and neglect |
| Help without doing it for you | Learning and parental help |
| A promotion and the people at home | Ambition and shared costs |
| A request that is also a boundary | Debt, support and limits |
| Work follows you home | Presence and availability |
| An object with an inconvenient story | Honest family history |
| Keeping a friendship across distance | Sustainable connection |
| The future is not a shared assumption | Partners with different plans |
| Advice your grown child did not ask for | Listening to adult children |
| An apology without a receipt | Accountability and repair |

Choices affect existing happiness, stress, closeness and trust systems. Outcomes
write persistent memories and schedule a branch-specific reflection one to three
years later. Reflections remain about the original person after their death or
relationship change; they never silently select another relative.

## Functionality and interface

- Stable person identities and known parent links survive living transfers,
  death succession and saving/loading. New births retain both known parents.
- A searchable family book retains historical people across generations. Open
  it through **More → Family & viewpoint → Family records**, the family tree,
  or **Personal record** on a person's screen.
- Records show occupation, personal cash, education and known qualifications, illness, convictions,
  known parents and the most recent 32 history entries. Missing old-save salary
  and schooling details are labelled as estimates; an unknown occupation stays
  unknown. No unrecorded college major or professional licence is invented.
- The child-transfer confirmation previews the child's own recorded life.
  Their personal history and identity stay with them. The parent remains alive,
  retains personal funds, and keeps a separate playable-state/story snapshot.
- Death succession preserves surviving relatives' stats, personal money and
  histories, remaps parent/spouse links, and retains the deceased parent's last
  recorded circumstances. A surviving spouse is not duplicated as a rival.
- Former players continue ageing and retain the NPC simulation's health and
  life changes. A once-per-year projected budget updates their money and their
  stored snapshot: recorded salary or pension, a flat 25% simulated tax, housing,
  remaining mortgage payments and $12,000 base living costs. A paid mortgage
  stops charging. This is a simplified background projection, not a second
  full player simulation; businesses, portfolios and queued personal decisions
  are not actively played out in that snapshot.
- **This year in my life** shows cash, health and happiness changes since ageing
  up, living children, scheduled consequences and recent log entries.
- The dark themes, varied panels, mature stories, confirmed fictional ending,
  four game modes and minigame refinements from v0.27 remain included.

## Boundaries of this milestone

The existing family-tree view gains a records route; a completely new genealogy
renderer is not claimed. Known lineage is retained; uncertain biological or
adoptive history cannot be reconstructed from old saves. Histories are compact,
not an unlimited autobiography. Ordinary NPCs retain the existing autonomy
model, with age-updated school scaffolds; they do not yet have the full planned
household, education, employment or investigation systems.

No new minigame is added in this milestone. Existing minigames are included and
checked again. The wider campaign's new games, careers, household systems and
original campaigns are future updates. This preview does not establish feature
parity or superiority over another game.
