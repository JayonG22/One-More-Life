# v0.32 — Work and Independence

Local Windows preview. This milestone includes v0.31.1 and retains the approved
completion roadmap. It does not complete v1.0 or claim full BitLife parity.

- Work → Work & independence groups projects, progression, training, hours,
  money and records. Navigation search includes the hub, projects and training.
- 89 listed occupations now have two specific tasks and two project briefs each
  (178 briefs). Eight new entry occupations cover appliance/bicycle repair,
  dispatch, technical support, recycling, groundskeeping, print and venue service.
- Projects require planning, an actual job scenario and handover. Each step costs
  one time; careful planning/checking costs another. One project starts per year;
  it normally expires after the following year. Rushing shortens the deadline.
  Skills, wellbeing, mentors and specialist training affect execution; shortcuts,
  missed deadlines and false claims damage the work record. False handovers can
  be audited the following year and their bonuses reclaimed.
- Repeat clients retain trust, which affects starting quality and bonuses.
  Successful work improves field skills, portfolio evidence, performance and
  hiring prospects. Bonuses are taxed and recorded without paying them twice.
  Records are bounded to 50 projects, 20 clients and 100 relationship memories.
- Employers have a mentor, rival and ally alongside existing workplace culture,
  politics, union, dismissal and references. Mentor feedback costs one time once
  per year and improves field skill and current project support. This is a work
  interaction; broad continuing relationship stories remain in v0.33.
- Promotions require two years in rank, performance 70+, practical skill and
  completed work samples. Automatic reviews and promotion requests share these
  requirements. Requests cost one time, once per year, and still require an opening.
  Specialist training improves project reliability; leadership improves reviews
  and teamwork. Each costs the local equivalent of $800 and one time.
- 28 training fields offer retraining; 11 also offer apprenticeship study.
  Training starts at 16+, costs $4,500/$1,200 adjusted locally and one time,
  requires two practical units and a three-question assessment costing two time.
  Retraining takes at least one year; apprenticeship takes two. Two correct
  assessment answers qualify. Failed assessments retain units for a later retry.
  Certificates help applications and eligible vocational diploma requirements;
  they do not replace regulated degrees, required majors, licences or age limits.
  These routes are qualification programmes, not guaranteed paid placements.
- Regular, shorter and overtime schedules trade pay against performance/stress.
  Permanent, flexible and three-year contracts have different pay factors.
  Fixed contracts renew with sufficient performance and employer health, or end
  after final wages. Probation affects references or a performance plan. Booked
  leave reduces the next birthday's wages by 10% and improves health/mood/stress.
- Money & recovery shows cash, savings, expected wages and personal-loan payments.
  Personal loans are serviced after wages and living bills. Extra repayment accepts
  a chosen amount without silently changing contracted annual payments.
  An optional buffer saves 5% of income after bills/loans while retaining a local
  $2,500 cash cushion. Job-search planning costs two time once per year, improves
  interviews and explains a CV gap without inventing work experience.
- Freelance net income and unemployment support now remain in the annual income
  breakdown. Existing borrowing, investments, businesses and ownership remain.

No configurable age jump was added: monthly infancy and yearly later ageing
remain. Dark themes, button distinctions, shopping categories and avatar artwork
remain. Broader specialised careers, business contracts, equipment, childhood
pathways and the rest of the accepted campaign remain open in the v1.0 plan.
