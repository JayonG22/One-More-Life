# v0.87 — School, Activities and Careers

An interim depth update. The grouped school/career milestone and the full
base-game acceptance remain open.

## Included

- **64 additional authored assessment questions**, two in each of the existing
  32 subjects. The bank now contains 128 questions across preschool,
  kindergarten, elementary, middle school, high school and college. Answers
  shuffle; spent questions do not become an unlimited reward loop.
- Class practice now builds a visible subject record as well as grades and
  attendance. Class pages show a progress bar. Overall performance and earlier
  records still carry between stages; this bar represents accumulated class
  work, not a separate diploma or an automatic pass.
- An incorrect assessment leaves a saved revision task. Reviewing it with a
  current teacher costs one time, records the correction, modestly improves
  grades and class progress, and clears that task. Repeating the same correction
  cannot earn another award. Each subject permits one review per year.
- Longer school projects recruit teachers and classmates from the current
  classroom. If someone becomes unavailable, replacement candidates also come
  from that school community; existing handover records remain.
- The principal and school nurse stay across grades within the same school
  stage, preserving their relationships. A new stage changes that staff; an
  unavailable staff member is replaced. Classroom casts still change by grade.
- Mentor coaching now offers an actual duty goal or feedback on an active
  project. Advice costs one time. Completing the coached duty costs its ordinary
  time, earns one field skill, changes mentor trust/respect, and creates a later
  named-person Connection. Advice alone no longer awards that skill.
- Coaching records retain the chosen duty, actual mentor, job session, deadline
  and conclusion. Goals expire after the following year; leaving the job ends
  its coaching. History is bounded to 40 entries. The goal can only pay once.
- **Eight new workplace dilemmas** for mechanic, electrician, carpenter,
  teacher, nurse, chef, developer and accountant. Each has three approaches,
  six specific success/partial outcomes, and a later review. These supplement
  the existing duties, projects and encounter pools.
- Relevant practical challenges now reward the current related career field:
  for example, care coordination can support Healthcare and kitchen service can
  support Hospitality. The saved attempt preserves that field and job session;
  changing jobs cannot redirect its performance reward. Other challenges keep
  their existing field. Scored practice retains its time, fee, yearly limit and
  60% skill threshold; free practice retains no rewards.
- Practice pages show personal bests and scored attempts. A probation
  performance plan can close at a later annual review once performance reaches
  55, rather than remaining permanently marked as failed.

## Still open

The question bank remains finite. This is the simplified default school
sequence, not a complete implementation of every national curriculum.
Revision keeps the latest pending mistake per subject; earlier answers remain
in the school decision record. School projects, clubs, cliques, teams and
leadership retain their earlier implementations and still need wider content
and owner acceptance.

Wider specialist duties, recurring client/mentor branches, career transitions,
sports and minigame refinement, adulthood integration and release gates remain
in [NEXT-WORK](../NEXT-WORK.md) and the
[four-update campaign](../FOUR-UPDATE-CAMPAIGN.md). No entire campaign row was
closed merely by increasing the version number.

No gameplay tests were performed, at the owner's request. See the
[delivery notes](V0.87-VALIDATION.md).
