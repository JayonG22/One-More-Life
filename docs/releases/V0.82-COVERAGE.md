# v0.82 — Lives Carry Forward

Interim development release, requested by the owner before completing v1.0.
All existing game modes remain. This package includes the work since v0.81.

- **Story Life:** twelve original campaigns; six additions replace the four
  inspected TV-based previews. Saved old-preview journals remain archived.
- **Faces:** child and older expressions follow the established Fluent artwork,
  with six skin tones and the existing cosmetics. Special themed adult hair
  still needs further expression work.
- **Education:** practical qualifications can lead into college with earned
  credit, lower fees and distinct capstones. Major changes and interrupted study
  retain their records. All 28 fields have distinct supervised placements,
  decisions and lasting evidence; a dishonest placement cannot earn a reference
  just by raising trust.
- **School:** ten clubs gain 20 situations and 60 outcomes. Plans affect their
  challenges and named classmates. Nine cliques gain 18 situations and 54
  outcomes, with choice-specific trust/popularity and later follow-ups. Authored
  decisions do not replay for the same person after exhaustion; ordinary
  practice and quiet meetups remain available.
- **Longer projects:** pause, return or close unfinished work without losing
  earlier choices. Actual teachers and classmates remain recorded, including
  handovers after losing a collaborator. Final presentations can finish at
  ages 18–20; student leadership starts at 13.
- **Generations:** executor work has claims, accounts and reporting stages.
  Former-player estates settle from that person's finances after a living
  transfer. Heirs do not inherit unpaid adult debts or receive the estate twice.
  Parent, child and sibling bereavement carries through saves and transfers.
- **Reliability:** saved dictionaries are copied independently, and an empty
  person identity no longer resolves to an unrelated family member.

The Windows application is portable and unsigned. Extract the ZIP and launch
**One More Life.exe**, with its PCK alongside it. No command launcher is needed.
Back up important saves before upgrading.

This does not complete the agreed base-game scope. See
[remaining work](../NEXT-WORK.md) and the
[completion register](../COMPLETION-REGISTER.md). No GitHub release has been
published and no project-wide reuse licence has been added.
