# v0.88.1 — Career Decisions

This continues the adult-life campaign. It does not close v0.88 or the feature-complete gate.

- Six speciality cases: workload, shared credit, delegation, unusual faults,
  quality checks and teaching a method. Each offers three plans, an immediate
  result and distinct success/failure reviews next year.
- Access requires the existing specialist/team-lead qualification, an active
  job and living colleague. Starting costs two time; one case per year. Cases
  do not repeat in the same life, including after a change of job or route.
- Choices affect performance, player/colleague stress and actual trust/respect.
  Readiness and field skill affect displayed chances. Saved rolls, decisions,
  colleague identities and job sessions persist in the employment record.
- Next-year review gives at most one field-skill point and modest reputation and
  performance changes. Job loss, custody or a changed/unavailable colleague ends
  the case without an award. Histories retain the conclusion; no cash bonus is added.
- Role & promotion links to Speciality decisions. Existing duties, coaching,
  qualifications and career projects remain available.
- An unavailable client contact can be replaced for one time in the matching
  career field. Company work history remains; trust moves toward neutral and the
  personal reference resets. The introduction uses that year's meeting allowance.
- New references require two completed projects witnessed by the current
  contact, trust 65+ and a successful request. Projects retain their original
  contact, preventing work begun for a predecessor from qualifying the replacement.
  Existing saved accounts retain their prior witnessed-work count until replaced.
- Client profiles and records are accessible beside meetings. Review choices
  appear in actual-person memories and bounded account histories. Exhausted
  meeting content explains why no new review opens, without spending time.
