## Reachability and preservation

Assets → Planning & commitments; Activities → Shopping → Vehicles & transport → Second Lap Autos; Settings → Save recovery; existing My household and Skills & competition panels. The dark interface, short wrapped buttons, navigation distinction, original avatar design, monthly infancy/yearly later progression, mature-content preferences and childhood household support remain. Earned child savings still survive independence; no time-jump slider is restored.

Retainers use existing saved client identities and finished-work evidence. Full scope takes two time and has higher success odds. Smaller scope takes one time with lower pay/odds. Unpaid renegotiation takes one time and reduces pressure without manufacturing income. Work must be performed each year; two misses terminate, and the three-year deadline closes the agreement. Three successful paid deliveries can earn a reference at sufficient trust. Existing career projects and new retainers are distinct commitments, not duplicate payment for the same project.

Used-car transactions preserve original vehicle age and mileage. An immediate resale pays less than its asking price. Service and repair may improve value through real costs and time; this is not a blanket guarantee that every repair is profitable. Listings are finite for the year and save with the person. The pool does not add new car models or icons.

New saves store the exact serialized payload with a checksum to avoid checksum drift during numeric parsing. Plain older saves remain loadable. Two backups and a previous transaction copy support recovery; explicit checkpoints stay until replaced. Checkpoints restore an earlier age with confirmation, while keeping the current life as a recovery copy. Save schema upgrades are forward-compatible with valid older files; downgrading to v0.50 is not supported for the new format. Shared scene familiarity and achievements are outside individual slot rollback.
