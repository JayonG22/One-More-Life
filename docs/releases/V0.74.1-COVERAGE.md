# v0.74.1 — Emoji Faces

The user's request for a more emoji-like appearance guides this visual patch.
Faces are larger and rounder, with larger dark eyes, wider smiles, softer brows
and simpler shading. The classic skin option is closer to emoji yellow.
The former bust is reduced to a small clothing accent. All skin tones, saved
hair and facial choices, accessories, age changes and health expressions remain.
Expressions are drawn into the face, with no separate status emoji.

The preceding v0.74 gameplay is included. This patch does not complete the
remaining gameplay work or the full themed-look review. Visual acceptance and
the full v1.0 completion gates remain open in the completion register.
