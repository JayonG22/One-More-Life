The focused v0.31 checks cover the original 24-month infancy calendar followed
by annual ageing, all seven firsts, displayed-decision recovery, bundle costs,
time, preferences, failed preflight and annual guards, household/work duties,
lifestyle sensitivity across all five stats, bounded monthly changes and saved
period guards. Both branches of each of the eight new callback scenes are
exercised, along with measured bond deltas and bounded history.

UI checks exercise search and favourites, actual destination buttons, Back and
scroll restoration, contextual bundle discovery/cost descriptions, mode-aware
guidance, recaps and consequence displays at normal and 175% text size. The
study bundle is clicked through the real game UI, its Play button and three
correct answers; no quiz grades appear before answering, and the actual result
improves grades afterward. Original v0.30 UI/Depth checks still verify avatar,
school, job tasks, loan controls and interactive challenge routes.

Existing mode, content, migration, accessibility, input, layout, performance,
long-life and minigame checks remain gates. The 40-game automated player
verification is distinct from the 42 release gates. These checks do not prove
complete balance, every possible combination or completion of the full campaign.

The engine reports its host certificate-store warning during startup. No script,
parse or gate failures remain in the included passing logs. Some headless checks
also report object-cleanup warnings at exit; those are not hidden as gate failures.

Two separate-mode checks exceeded the initial 300-second process deadline under
the combined workload. Both completed with the same assertions and a longer
deadline. The busy parallel performance run exceeded its unchanged 250 ms/year
limit; a later run recorded 134 ms/year and passed. No performance assertion was
weakened. The full default simulation completed 80 lives and 4,817 years.
