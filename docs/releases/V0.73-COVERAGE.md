# v0.73 — Familiar Faces

Development checkpoint; the full accepted v1.0 scope remains open.

## Emoji-like portraits

The user's correction to preserve the previous style governs this pass: round
heads, simple dark oval eyes, softly rounded hair, warm skin shading and the
established circular bust composition. Facial expressions are drawn into the
person's eyes, eyebrows and mouth, with complexion reflecting low health.
There is no separate facial-status emoji. The existing neutral setting remains.
Age changes hair colour and subtle facial lines; individual face choices persist.

Appearance adds 91 free options, including defaults, across 12 categories:
haircut, hair colour, eyes, eye colour, brows, nose, mouth, face shape, facial
details, facial hair, accessories and clothing colour. Existing skin/figure/look,
background/rim/charm indices and paid ownership are retained in older saves.
Legacy hair choices remain selectable. Optional themed looks still use their
saved indices; complete visual equivalence across all 38 looks is not claimed.

NPCs receive saved facial choices tied to their stable identity. Human relationship
rows, person pages and family trees draw those faces; animals retain their own
icons. Reloading and switching to a living child preserve the actual child's
appearance. Compact textual/header emoji references remain where appropriate;
a full audit of every special human mode and themed costume remains open.

## Brief wording

26 authored screen summaries support inline Details for explanations longer than
180 characters on mapped screens. Brief factual lines and costs remain visible.
The full explanation remains available without a second modal. Unmapped screens
retain their explanations pending review. Appearance, Identity and company
introductions are also shortened. This is a readability increment, not a claim
that every label or description across the whole game has been audited.

The company operations and succession work from v0.72 remains included, alongside
all earlier accepted systems. The completion register retains every open gate.
