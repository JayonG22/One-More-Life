# v0.41 — Stories That Carry Forward

Local Windows preview, 2 October 2026. This is the first implementation of the
[depth and replayability priorities](../DEPTH-AND-REPLAYABILITY.md), building on
v0.40. It is not the completed v1.0 base game or a published GitHub release.

## What changes in play

- **Shared novelty history:** optional library events, age scenes, curriculum
  lessons, graded quiz questions and workplace encounters use saved personal
  history. Optional authored scenes are not recycled within that person’s life.
  Identical source question text is recognized even through another activity.
  Local shared history prioritizes unfamiliar scenes and story families across
  new lives and save slots. Required consequences, major arcs and system notices
  retain their normal behavior. The shared record lives alongside existing meta
  progress, not in one particular save slot.
- **No forced age-scene recycling:** exhausting the eligible age-scene pool leaves
  a quiet period. Selected age scenes only count as experienced when displayed;
  an optional scene removed by the yearly popup budget is not falsely consumed.
- **64 new curriculum questions:** sixteen distinct problems per age band,
  covering ages 5–8, 9–11, 12–14 and 15–17. The two lesson buttons draw from
  an eligible pool, rather than two fixed questions repeated each year. Selected
  questions and answer order remain in saved prompts.
- **Graded subject pools:** those same 64 questions also expand the existing
  Arithmetic, Reading, Science and Civics banks, with age gates. They are shared
  content, not counted as another 64 authored problems. The shared text history
  prevents a curriculum question immediately returning through the quiz menu.
  Three unseen eligible questions are required for a graded assessment. When a
  pool is exhausted, the game explains it without charging time; practical
  exercises remain available. Existing Memory exercises are retained.
- **Distinct school projects:** fifteen separately written stages across science,
  arts, community reading, team sport and student leadership. Choices trade
  potential quality against stress. Readiness and relevant practice affect actual
  quality; very low readiness can finish below the award threshold. Each project
  type is undertaken once per life, including abandoned projects. Previous saved
  project prompts and their answer rules remain supported.
- **118 workplace dilemmas:** one particular situation for each of 89 occupations,
  plus 29 professional-field situations. Two annual work-encounter buttons draw
  from the new situations and retained occupation task checks. The original 178
  task checks still support vocational training; they are not claimed as newly
  rewritten dilemmas.
- **Work has follow-through:** the new dilemmas offer effort, coordinated help
  and limited-scope approaches. Readiness, relevant skill, school portfolios,
  professional standing and overtime affect execution. The execution roll is
  stored with the selected prompt. Effort costs health/stress; support uses
  professional goodwill; compromise limits the gain. Employees do not pay their
  employer’s staffing costs out of their own wallets. A next-year review settles
  reputation and, if still in the same job session, performance. A linked living
  colleague can remember the work through trust/respect changes.
- **17 personal life stories:** maps and classroom models, performances, reading
  spaces, club priorities, team transport, placements, departing friends, budgets,
  reunions, hobbies, family scheduling, later learning, mentoring, familiar places,
  car ownership and home space. The stories cover childhood through later life,
  with eligibility for car/home-specific situations. Each has three authored,
  choice-specific conclusions: **51 conclusions, not 51 additional starting
  stories**. Costs and later effects differ. Conclusions settle automatically the
  following year and appear in the life log and connected-life journal.
- **Personal continuity:** new story conclusions and work reviews can settle for
  a former player after switching to a child. Rewards belong to that person.
  Their remaining scheduled story endpoint is removed after settlement. Death
  closes outstanding records without inventing a completed outcome or award.
- **125 distinguishable vector icons:** 89 occupation tool combinations, 25
  educational-field combinations and 11 vehicle-model drawings. They extend the
  original muted vector style. Job listings, work encounters, major selection,
  vehicle shopping and owned-vehicle display use the new identifiers. This is
  not a claim that every existing emoji/menu icon has been replaced.
- **Clearer journal:** open and concluded personal stories are listed alongside
  the existing connected-life entries; a new life gets a useful empty-state
  message instead of an empty panel.

## Where to find it

- More → Connected lives → Growing up: new curriculum and school projects.
- Work → Occupation: Shift encounter and Another work encounter.
- Work → School/University: graded quizzes and educational pathways.
- More → Connected lives → Identity → My connected-life journal: consequences.
- Shopping → Vehicles: individual model icons; education/job listings also use
  individual symbols. New life stories join the normal eligible event library.

## What remains open

Finite pools can be exhausted. New lives prioritize unseen local content, then
the least familiar eligible content; unlimited uniqueness is not claimed.
Deleting/resetting shared meta progress removes its cross-save memory.

This pass does not yet route every fixed prompt through shared selection.
Vocational assessments, several minigame scenario sets, enterprise/community
stages and separate-mode stories still need wider authored pools and deeper
consequence chains. Existing occupation task-check wrong answers still need
individual review. Club/team seasons, NPC motives, comprehensive vehicle
reliability/mileage, deeper property obligations, mature investigations and
broader career-specific challenges remain on the accepted campaign.

School projects now differ by type, but they still have one three-stage sequence
per type rather than several alternative seasons. The 17 new stories have
choice-dependent automatic conclusions, not multi-year interactive campaigns.
Workplace outcomes share execution mechanics; long profession-specific case
arcs and employer economics need further depth. Icons share a coherent tool
grammar and do not constitute a new avatar or full replacement icon library.

The [completion roadmap](../V1.0-PLAN.md) and
[ongoing register](../UPDATE-REGISTER.md) remain authoritative. This release
advances the depth audit without marking the complete accepted campaign finished.
