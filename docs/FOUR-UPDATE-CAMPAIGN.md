# Completion campaign

Approved on 3 October 2026 as an organized delivery sequence. v0.89 established
unified integration work; v0.90 and v0.91 have local review packages, and v0.92
is active. Its scope is still open.
This plan groups the open scope; it does not delete
completed work, reduce the accepted 180-item campaign, or mark any milestone
complete in advance.

The separate **Growing Up** and **Skills & Competition** destinations stay
retired. Their useful activities remain in School, Work, Sports, and the relevant
personal activity pages. Each update should shorten routes and connect existing
systems before adding a new top-level destination.

## v0.86 — People and Lasting Stories

**Goal:** Choices, people, and event history continue to matter across the life.

- Expand event variety by age and current circumstances; favor unfamiliar scenes
  across lives and save slots without hiding content when a finite pool is spent.
- Make meaningful choices leave readable memories, relationship changes, and
  follow-ups that resolve, expire, or adapt when people are unavailable.
- Complete the open people branches: motives, recurring friends and rivals,
  reconciliation, long-distance ties, promises, and childhood callbacks.
- Show simple relationship cues in the relevant panel; keep optional NPC profiles
  for deeper detail.
- Keep developmental firsts distinct, single-use milestones with their existing
  achievement hooks.

**Acceptance:** A scene is not considered complete if it only changes prose: its
choice, cost or chance, person/stat outcome, persistence, and any later callback
must agree. Loading a pending scene must preserve the same prompt.

## v0.87 — School, Activities, and Careers

**Goal:** Education and work provide legible, distinct paths with lasting value.

- Complete stage-appropriate classes, assessments, attendance, grade progression,
  school-team selection, talent rounds, club projects, cliques, and student
  campaigns. Carry useful outcomes into later education and opportunities.
- Finish distinctive job duties and events, recurring clients and mentors,
  specializations, contracts, probation, leave, retraining, and management paths.
- Connect preparation, qualifications, stats, readiness, costs, and outcomes in
  compact feedback.
- Improve existing sports and contextual minigames for clear instructions,
  fair rewards, short repeat attempts, and accessible input.
- Keep all of this inside the existing School, Activities, and Work sections.

**Acceptance:** School and career actions must explain the requirement or cost,
change an appropriate record or relationship, and produce an opportunity or
trade-off that persists beyond the current screen.

## v0.88 — A Whole Life with Consequences

**Goal:** Adult life, difficult years, and later generations use the same coherent
rules as childhood and work.

- Connect household money, shared budgets, debt recovery, mortgages, education
  costs, leases, housing, transport, maintenance, and estate settlement.
- Extend treatment history, access to care, recovery, caregiving, and later-life
  support across relevant modes.
- Connect relationships, parenting, blended families, custody, actual-child
  continuity, family businesses, succession, and contested estates.
- Deepen enterprise, creative work, politics, royalty, migration, local
  institutions, community projects, and quiet-life goals through persistent
  obligations and outcomes.
- Complete connected investigations, trials, prison and re-entry, with mature
  content preferences and coherent aftermath.
- Complete approved original Story Life campaigns and integrate their endings and
  journals with ordinary life continuity.

**Acceptance:** Ownership, money, health, family, and legal outcomes must remain
consistent through ageing, transfers, death, and save/load. Dramatic paths must
include consequences and recovery routes, not only a one-screen result.

## v0.89 — One More Life, Unified

**Goal:** Remove the remaining seams between systems and prepare an honest release
candidate.

The current work order gives priority to closing partial implementations from
earlier updates before adding fresh feature scope. Track those carryovers in the
[prior-update closeout board](FEATURE-CLOSEOUT-BOARD.md).

- Reconcile every accepted request against the completion register; finish missing
  branches and expose contradictions rather than silently dropping them.
- Apply one navigation and panel standard throughout: concise wording, visible
  costs and destinations, consistent Back behavior, quiet dark themes, distinct
  bulk actions, and readable lists at supported display sizes.
- Finish original avatar coverage, contextual icons, audio controls, accessibility,
  and mode consistency within the established visual style.
- Review balance across ordinary, wealthy, difficult, quiet, and unusual lives;
  resolve save migration, recovery, family-scale performance, credits, packaging,
  and public project descriptions.

**Acceptance:** v0.89 can close feature rows only when their actual behavior and
connections are complete. The v0.90–v0.99 release-candidate gates remain for
owner playthrough, defect closure, package/platform review, and truthful release
claims. v1.0 remains blocked until all accepted requirements and release gates
are satisfied.

## v0.90 — Candidate Readiness & Lasting Context

**Goal:** start the local-review checkpoint sequence with more contextual
choices and clearer continuity, while carrying all unfinished v0.89 and earlier
scope forward.

- Add short, age-appropriate school, work and community stories with named people,
  direct record/relationship effects and delayed callbacks.
- Reconcile open requirements and public claims, then close blockers through
  owner-led play and release review before creating a candidate package.
- Preserve the established interface and original character style; keep stories
  brief and their costs visible.

**Acceptance:** source changes alone do not establish a release candidate. The
owner's gameplay acceptance, blocker closure, compatibility review and package
inspection remain required.

## v0.91 — School & Career Paths

**Goal:** give school and work distinct, connected progression while preserving
the existing section layout.

- Complete age-appropriate academic progress, school communities, clubs, cliques,
  teams, talent and leadership pathways.
- Connect preparation and qualifications to applications, apprenticeships and
  later opportunities.
- Expand occupation-specific duties, mentors, clients and practical advancement;
  explain the effect of stats and choices.

**Acceptance:** activities change school or employment records and lead to a
concrete next opportunity, requirement or trade-off. v0.91 queues graduation at
the diploma transition, previews school-project admission support, and shows
job-fit factors before applying. Broader foundations remain open in the
[completion register](COMPLETION-REGISTER.md).

## v0.92 — People & Relationships

**Goal:** make the people around the player legible at a glance and consequential
over time.

- Keep relationship summaries simple, with optional detailed NPC profiles.
- Finish immediate-family, friend, rival, romantic, past and deceased pathways;
  deepen trust, promises, reconciliation, boundaries and co-parenting.
- Preserve callbacks when people move, die, separate or change roles.

**Acceptance:** meaningful decisions change a named relationship or a recorded
follow-up, with alternatives when the original participant is unavailable.

## v0.93 — Activities & Competition

**Goal:** make hobbies, sports, fights and existing minigames understandable,
varied and connected to life progression.

- Complete tactical sport and martial-arts progression, practice feedback and
  fair repeat attempts.
- Review the 39 existing minigames for clear controls, short instructions,
  keyboard access, assisted options and rewards tied to relevant preparation.
- Add contextual challenges inside existing School, Work and activity pages.

**Acceptance:** each activity tells the player what it develops, what it costs
and how the result affects a saved record or opportunity.

## v0.94 — Health, Crime & Recovery

**Goal:** connect difficult adult paths to their human consequences and routes
back to stability.

- Link health histories, providers, treatment, caregiving and later-life needs.
- Deepen mature relationship stories under the chosen content preferences.
- Connect crime choices to investigation, evidence, trials, sentencing, prison,
  appeal and re-entry without instructional crime detail.
- Keep the confirmed suicide ending non-graphic and without a success reward.

**Acceptance:** serious paths have clear consequences and recovery alternatives;
preferences do not erase obligations or callbacks already in progress.

## v0.95 — Money, Homes & Ownership

**Goal:** keep household finances and owned property consistent from childhood
through independent life and inheritance.

- Reconcile wages, benefits, bills, debt, savings, household transfers and net
  worth in the same ledger.
- Make childhood costs household-supported where appropriate; apply the actual
  country's independence age and obligations when a young adult begins their own
  budget.
- Connect regional store stock, housing, vehicles, upkeep, leases, debt recovery
  and resale to the Shopping and Assets sections.

**Acceptance:** every purchase and recurring cost shows its blocker or price and
matches the saved ownership and ledger state.

## v0.96 — Enterprise, World & Modes

**Goal:** give ambitious, unusual and ordinary paths distinct responsibilities
inside one consistent life system.

- Complete deeper business, creative, political, royal and community decisions.
- Connect region, migration, local institutions and changing opportunities to
  household and career choices.
- Finish species-specific pet paths, prison/guard roles, special lives and the
  approved original Story Life catalog.

**Acceptance:** each mode has meaningful progression, consequences and a readable
ending that respects family, history and save continuity where applicable.

## v0.97 — Parenting & Generations

**Goal:** make family continuity work across childhood, adulthood, care, old age
and player transfers.

- Expand independent child development, blended families, custody and adult-child
  relationships.
- Connect retirement, later-life activities, care needs, estate planning,
  inheritance and heirlooms.
- Preserve the actual child's life and obligations when the player transfers.

**Acceptance:** several generations retain distinct histories, relationships,
property and unresolved duties without duplicating relatives or money.

## v0.98 — Interface, Art & Accessibility

**Goal:** complete the whole-game presentation using the approved original art
and compact, familiar navigation.

- Audit every panel for concise wording, clear costs, consistent Back behavior,
  distinct bulk/navigation buttons and calm dark themes.
- Extend the established avatar drawing with the requested faces and cosmetics;
  add contextual icons and distinctive minigame visuals in the same style.
- Complete sound, display, reduced-motion, keyboard and help controls across
  modes.

**Acceptance:** no section depends on a legacy navigation pattern or visually
overloaded panel; supported settings remain readable throughout.

## v0.99 — Release Candidate & Owner Review

**Goal:** prepare one accurate candidate package and close blockers found by the
owner's playthrough.

- Reconcile the full completion register and accepted 180-item campaign.
- Review save upgrades, backups, long-family performance, platform/export
  behavior, credits, asset provenance and the public project description.
- Provide a clearly labelled local candidate for the owner to play; collect and
  fix release blockers before v1.0.

**Acceptance:** the owner has completed the representative play review, every
accepted blocker is closed or explicitly brought back for scope agreement, and
the package and documentation make only verified claims.

### Checkpoint delivery policy

Each v0.9x milestone is a substantial local development checkpoint, not an
automatic public release. Once its scope is ready, prepare a local package for
the owner's play review. The development agent will not run gameplay tests or
simulations. v1.0 is the final package after acceptance; rights, credits and
public claims must be reviewed before any public release.
