# Coverage of “Understand Game Process”

This checklist includes the original refinement request and the later TVLife,
presentation, timeline, life-path and normal-mode prison requests. It distinguishes
implemented systems from the current amount of authored content.

| Request | v0.26 implementation | Current scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Place, weather, season, age, lifestyle, career and health relevance | Saved annual weather, explicit context filters, existing age/health conditions and 111 new contextual scenarios | Authored filters select eligible scenes; they do not guarantee a scene every year |
| Controlled annual events and fresh runs | Optional popup budget, theme variety, five-year repeat exclusion, restoration of shelved-event history | Required legal/family decisions and critical consequences can exceed the optional budget |
| Life-changing wildcards | Existing Twists preserved with a minimum five-year separation | Random twists supplement contextual progression |
| Education and employment | 24 additional jobs, relevant major/field mappings, practical courses, stable job boards and employer-specific hiring | Regulated qualifications remain required; preparation improves odds without guaranteeing a job |
| Payouts, chances, strategies and betting | Reviewed casino games, corrected hidden luck and payout rules, custom stakes, next-round stake changes | Existing interactive minigame strategies remain; no claim that every possible strategy has been exhaustively balanced |
| Investments and charts | 12 additional stocks/funds, four cryptocurrencies, migration and recorded yearly charts | Charts show actual simulated history, capped at 24 observations |
| TVLife | Four categories, four character arcs, 32 ordered chapters and a persistent reflection journal | A first catalog of selected arcs, not an exhaustive library or every episode of each series |
| Independent lore | Recurring local people and projects progress with or without help; demand and setbacks affect outcomes | One local development per year, with persistent history per setting/place |
| Timelines, “Become a” paths and special careers | Availability metadata, incompatible-world filters, priority for setting/work scenes and 27 additional scenes | Covers all three preset timelines, seven special human life paths and all 11 special careers; existing career systems remain active |
| Music, effects and Age button | Additional Fieldnotes/TVLife music themes, contextual sound and animated Age feedback | Procedural audio, not a recorded original soundtrack |
| Own visual identity and matte surfaces | Fieldnotes palette, revised four-mode title screen and matte bars without gloss/glints | Uses the existing layout and emoji artwork with a new presentation |
| Avatar customization, vanity and Star Shop | 12 additional looks, eight backdrops, previews and Star prices | Emoji-based customization, not a new illustrated avatar renderer |
| Normal-mode prison UI | Custody banner, remaining sentence, inmate label, muted palette and prison sound | Existing prison mechanics continue; release restores normal presentation |

The Claude audit attachment was read as historical reference. Existing roadmap
features were preserved rather than being treated as new, unfinished requirements.
Validation and package limitations are recorded in `VALIDATION-v0.26.0.md`.
