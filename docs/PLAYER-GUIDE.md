# Player guide

## Starting a life

Extract the Windows package, then open **One More Life.exe**. Keep its `.pck`
file beside the application. Developers can also import `project.godot` into Godot 4.4.1.
Choose a mode on the title screen, then start a new life or continue a save.
The application includes Human Life, Pets Life, Prison Life and original Story Life.

In human lives, press **Age** to advance one month before age two and one year afterward. Read the log, resolve decisions, and spend your
time on activities, people, work and possessions. Each mode has different needs
and endings. A good outcome is not guaranteed by choosing a particular career
or building a high stat.

## Finding things in v0.85

The six main tabs stay consistent: Activities, People, Work, Assets, Life and
More. People opens counted pages for close family, partners, pets, children,
contacts and past relationships. Assets groups money, home, transport, things
and ventures. Use **Activities → Shopping** to browse homes and vehicles.
**Work → Find a job** covers ordinary openings; **Careers & business** holds
special careers, creator work and companies. More keeps search, help, stories,
family tools, goals, settings and saves in short sections.

## Money and payment support

Open **Work → Money & recovery → Payment support**, or find Payment support on
the Borrow money screen. Choose a lender to see the balance and next payment.
Independent adults with loss of work, limited cash or poor health can request a
review. It costs one time and adds a 2% fee (minimum $1) to that loan.

Half payments last two birthdays. A pause lasts one birthday; the bank and credit
union offer it, while the online lender does not. The original payment resumes
afterwards. Longer term adds three years to the remaining contract and divides
the outstanding balance and fee over that schedule. No debt is forgiven. Each
loan allows one arrangement. The informal lender offers none.

Loan record keeps dated agreements, actual repayments and missed payments.
Early payments reduce the balance; annual payments remain due until it is cleared.
Supported children keep household protection. Transferring to a child keeps that
child's recorded debts and credit; it does not give them the parent's loans.

## Career projects

Open Work → Occupation → Projects. Twelve professions have three refined projects
each. Choose how to plan, do the work and report it. Detailed checks cost extra
time. During execution, buttons show the quality gain on success and the chance.
Safer choices usually gain less quality. Stats, field skill and mentor support
affect results. Work record keeps your choices, quality and bonuses.

These refined projects occur once per life. Practice and training remain available
when the set is exhausted. Strong work supports the existing promotion requirements;
a good result does not guarantee an opening. False reports can cost the original
bonus and professional reputation at a later review.

## Connected lives from v0.41

School lessons and work encounters draw different eligible scenes. The game
remembers optional scenes within a life and prefers unfamiliar content across
local save slots. If a pool runs out, it explains the limit without charging for
an old encounter. Ordinary work, practice, projects and annual systems remain
available. Reloading preserves selected prompts.

Find work encounters under **Occupation → Your job → Workplace situations**.
Open **Occupation → Education → School community** for your grade, classroom,
classes, school events and projects. Practical practice is kept with work,
household finance, martial arts and the producer studio. Personal stories and
work reviews have later conclusions; check Identity → My connected-life journal.
Jobs, educational fields and vehicle models use their own original vector icons.
See [the exact additions and limits](releases/V0.41-COVERAGE.md).

Open **More → Life activities** for school, work, people, health and free time.
More help, personal records, family tools, goals and saves are grouped in their
own short pages. Each action shows its time/cash requirements.
Promises have deadlines; long projects need multiple steps and sometimes years.
Finish a current choice or challenge before ageing or changing viewpoints.

Practice challenges are free and give no progress. Scored attempts use time and
materials and are limited each year. New challenges are untimed: choose with
**1–3** or a button, then press **Enter** or **Continue**. Feedback follows each
round. Assisted resolution uses relevant readiness and skill.

Living child transfers preserve each person's ownership. Gifts and company
handovers actually remove the item from the giver. Inheritance follows death
and records the cash/asset transaction. A memoir remains readable by children.

For original campaigns, choose **TVLife → Original** on the main menu. Choices
branch toward alternate endings; reference stories retain their fixed order.
Identity offers optional-theme settings and a quiet-audio cap. Volume sliders
and motion/display settings remain available in Settings.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Space | Advance by the normal age step, or activate the selected button during keyboard navigation |
| 1–6 | Switch the six main tabs |
| Tab / arrows | Enter keyboard navigation and move focus |
| Enter / Space | Activate the focused control or acknowledge a result |
| Esc / Backspace | Go back in a menu |
| 1–9 during an event | Select an available choice |

A mouse click returns control to the mouse. Minigames show their own controls.
Adjust sound, motion, contrast and interface size in Settings.

## Saves

The game autosaves during play and provides save slots. Saves and settings live
in Godot's per-user application data location, outside the repository. Back up
that data before testing a new development build. Migration tests cover selected
older formats; they cannot guarantee every historical save will work.

## Reporting problems

Use the repository's [issues page](https://github.com/JayonG22/One-More-Life/issues).
Include the version, mode, operating system, what you selected, and what happened.
Remove personal information from screenshots and logs before sharing them.

## v0.27 preview navigation

The title menu groups Challenges, Rewards and Preferences beneath the four game
modes. TVLife provides genre filters and search; original character names also
find the new playful aliases.

During a minigame, **Pause / How to play** suspends the active board. Use
**Resume game** to continue. Leaving an unfinished wager loses that wager; settled
session winnings are retained. An ended non-casino attempt receives a low result.

In a human family life, **More → Family & viewpoint → Switch to a child** changes
the active character without killing the parent or transferring an estate. Choose
a living child and confirm. Their recorded age, stats, schooling, job and money
carry over. Resolve the current decision or game first. Duplicate the save from
**Your Lives** before switching if you want a separate branch.

For adult family characters, **More → Life decisions → End this life** opens a
confirmation for a non-graphic suicide ending. **Keep living** cancels; confirmation
closes the current life. Ordinary post-death child succession remains available.

All themes are dark. The saved Light theme is now presented as **Twilight**.
Settings can disable **Additional mature arcs**; consequences already started
still resolve. The game includes other mature themes regardless of this setting.


## v0.28 family records

**More → Family & viewpoint → Family records** opens the searchable family book.
Search by name, choose a person and inspect their recorded circumstances and
recent memories. The family tree also links to the book, and a person's screen
offers **Personal record**. Records remain available after a person dies or you
continue the family through a child. Older inferred salary/school details are
marked as estimates.

**This year in my life** in the same section shows changes since the last age-up
and recent log entries. Scheduled reflections stay attached to the person and
decision that started them. When switching to a child, inspect the preview:
their own age, cash, schooling and work are the starting point.

## v0.29 venues, creators and household

**Occupation → Business → Venues & ventures** lets adults aged 21+ open a casino,
museum or intelligence agency. Hire staff, maintain condition, add rooms or
exhibits, or recruit and train operatives. Annual reports show revenue, costs and
profit. Earnings stay in the venue reserve until withdrawn; losses can need your
personal cash. Sale requires confirmation. The Velvet Society is a paid social
membership with annual dues, gatherings and community grants.

**Occupation → Find work → Producer studio & CloudCrowd** lets adults choose a
genre, collaborate, mix through the existing rhythm minigame and release music.
Keep royalties or confirm a one-time license sale. **OnlyPals** offers non-graphic
adult subscription work: set fees and privacy, publish at most three times yearly
and inspect net earnings. Closing stops future subscription billing.

**More → My household & week** explains the last household bill, dependents,
roommate sharing and routine presets. Complete chores using time or choose an
annual paid service; rest reduces fatigue. These costs are part of the recorded
bills, not charged again when you inspect them.


## Childhood and gameplay depth in v0.30

Human infancy uses **One month** until age two, then **One year**. First milestones
appear once; annual bills and other people advance on birthdays. New age scenes
are drawn separately. Optional scenes respect the event-density setting.

**Occupation → Education** has subject quizzes and practical exercises. School
life → **School challenges & portfolio** offers club projects, a clique gathering,
team fixtures, talent entries and a class-president campaign. The election winner
can chair a council meeting and fund a project. Participation stays in your save
and can help relevant careers and ordinary hiring. Grades also affect admission,
scholarships and qualifications. Each activity costs time and has yearly limits.

**Occupation** exposes two specific tasks for your current ordinary job. Make a
decision about its actual work; the outcome changes performance, rather than
granting an immediate promotion. More → **What my stats affect** explains readiness,
and **Choices I remember** shows your persistent choice history.

**Martial arts** offers classes, supervised sparring and belt assessments. Train
to 100% readiness, then pass the assessment. Higher belts unlock tactics with
different stamina, counter and defence trade-offs. **Pro-athlete big games** ask
for a late-game plan before the playable clutch moment.

**Legal → Lawsuit** asks for grounds and representation, then disclosure,
cross-examination and closing arguments. A verdict records costs and damages.
**Crime → Murder** is a non-graphic fictional action with a confirmation choice
and lasting legal/relationship consequences.

**Borrow money → Configure this loan** accepts an exact whole amount or a decimal
percentage of the lender's limit, and an eligible term. Read the live repayment
quote before signing. Currency uses whole units and the game's flat-interest model.

Hovering over the original portrait shows a life-condition hint. Appearance
adds looks, dark backgrounds, rims and charms. Settings → **Ask activity length
each time** can be switched off to repeat each everyday activity's saved duration.


## v0.31 convenience, lifestyle, search and consequences

Human infancy advances monthly until age two, then yearly. The existing infant
allowance and annual time budget remain. Separate pet/prison/guard modes use
annual progression; TVLife uses chapters. The configurable jump was removed.

Paid **bulk buttons** combine activities in Mind & Body, Entertainment, Education,
Health & Looks, your current occupation and Household. Each shows its full time
cost and total price, including a convenience fee. Each bundle is limited to
once per year, and cannot bypass individual activity restrictions. The guided
study bundle still requires answering the actual quiz to earn quiz grades.

All five stats change with **lifestyle** each month in infancy and each year later.
Consistent movement, learning, relaxation, enjoyment and grooming help; stress,
illness, poor sleep/diet and age can worsen the trajectory. High stats require
maintenance. Infancy uses parent closeness, illness and home conditions. Annual
changes are larger than monthly ones. More → Consequences & changes explains
the actual changes; annual lifestyle changes also appear in your life log.

**Find & favourites** searches activities and important destinations. Results
show where they live and whether they are currently available. Opening a result
does not perform the action. Tap **☆** to save a shortcut, then use Favourites.
Back remembers the previous panel's scroll position; Home returns to the tab root.
The **?** button explains the current mode.

**More → Consequences & changes** shows scheduled follow-ups and measured recent
effects. **What my stats affect** explains current readiness and its inputs.
**This year in my life** groups recent changes by topic. Selected school/teen
decisions now return in distinct later scenes quoting the original choice;
this does not mean every recorded decision already has a bespoke follow-up.


## Shopping and buttons

**OPEN** takes you to another panel. **BUNDLE** combines activities at the shown
time and cost, including a fee. Other action buttons do what their label says.
Menu names and descriptions wrap; use the scrollbar for longer lists.

Go to **Activities → Shopping** to browse stores, homes, vehicles and animals.
Stores have different products. To buy a home or car, use Shopping; to sell or
manage one you own, use Assets. Investment rentals are still in Assets.
Choose an animal breed before buying or adopting; the card shows yearly upkeep.


## Combined v0.43–v0.50 preview

- Assets → Ownership & everyday use: car condition, mileage, resale, servicing and repair; home capacity, utilities and commuting.
- Work → Occupation → Clients: review relationships with identifiable contacts. Two finished projects, trust 65+ and a successful reference request can help future applications in the same field. Deceased contacts cannot supply references.
- Assets → My museum (age 21+): pay the fit-out, install owned objects, research available provenance, set admission and maintain the venue. Annual attendance depends on exhibits, condition and price. Costs continue even with free admission or no visitors. Closing returns exhibits but not the fit-out payment. An adult child can take over.
- More → Connected lives → Ambitions & enterprise: three distinct commitments per kind, each with planning, delivery and accountability. Readiness, suppliers, pricing, credits and deadlines change outcomes. These authored commitments do not repeat within one life.
- Baker, mortician, paranormal investigator/exorcist, Marines, Coast Guard and adult performer have ranks, knowledge tasks, dilemmas and projects. The adult career starts at 21 and follows the mature-content setting; content is non-graphic.

Monthly infancy and annual later progression remain. This combined preview is not the completed v1.0 base game.


### Childhood money

Assets → Family budget shows family-covered costs and assistance. Children keep earned money and gifts, can contribute cash to living parents from age six, and do not inherit routine event cash deficits. Support ends at 18 unless the household agreement chooses 16 or 21. Adult expenses begin after support ends. This is a game agreement, not local legal advice. Buying optional items still requires your own affordable cash; independence does not delete savings.


## v0.60 Sustainable Lives

- Assets → Planning & commitments: estimate ordinary annual bills and work/pension income. The forecast excludes unpredictable income, optional spending and loan repayments; it never charges you.
- Choose 0%, 10%, 20% or 30% of annual surplus to move into savings after bills. It creates no extra money and never borrows to save.
- Planning & commitments → Recurring client agreements: two completed projects and trust 65+ unlock a three-year retainer. At most two run together. Choose a full delivery, smaller scope or unpaid renegotiation each year. Time, readiness, actual work and trust affect payments. Misses have consequences; old employer agreements cannot be delivered from a new job. Contacts can die and former-player commitments continue to expire.
- Activities → Shopping → Vehicles & transport → Second Lap Autos: persistent annual used-car listings. Age, mileage and asking price are visible; paid inspection reveals condition without rerolling it. A purchase credits your old car's actual resale value and preserves the bought car's wear.
- Household fatigue now reflects work hours, commute and young dependents. Fatigue above 25 lowers readiness; rest, shorter hours, practical travel and household services can help manage pressure. This is game balance, not a medical prediction.
- Settings → Save recovery: keep a checkpoint and restore it with confirmation. Two rolling backups and the previous write are also retained. Automatic recovery can return to an earlier age. Restoring a checkpoint preserves current progress as a recovery copy.

Existing plain saves are accepted and upgraded on the next save. The new checked save format is not intended for older game versions. Copy important saves before upgrading. A checksum detects accidental damage; it is not encryption or anti-cheat. Save failures show a brief notice and keep the previous file when replacement cannot complete. Manual checkpoint restore replays an older life; shared local scene familiarity remains shared.


## Continuing lives — v0.70

More → Connected lives includes Teams & seasons, Learning & placements, and
Hobbies & quiet goals. School sport selection helps season entry; three fixtures
use tactics, stamina and preparation. Community/part-time/distance courses retain
units when paused, then assess practical evidence. They do not replace degrees.
Placements need two supervised annual shifts before their deadline.

People → a person shows their current goal, useful support, earned favours and

hold each person's separate contribution; withdrawal returns only your share.

## Fresh Start and the application

Extract the Windows package, then double-click One More Life.exe. Keep its PCK
resource file beside it. Fresh Start on the main menu offers adult lives with a
paid rental deposit, a cash cushion and a scenario board. Four annual chapters
help shape the life; three steady years finish the scenario. Continue normal
work, friendships and learning between chapters. Second act needs a completed
practical course or work-training certificate. A bigger small world needs a
living friend at 60+ closeness. You can continue the life after finishing.

Story Life opens on the six original campaigns. Small Hours and The Borrowed
House join the earlier four; their choices lead to different endings. A loss-theme
preference can hide The Borrowed House. Earlier reference stories remain separately
categorized, and older saved stories remain supported.


### Running a company

Open **Occupation → Business → Company operations** after founding a company.
Choose a product/service plan, supplier, prices, staffing budget and capacity in
their own sections. Each change costs 1 time and can be made once per year per
setting. Premium pricing requires quality 70+ to avoid an extra demand penalty.
Cheaper supply saves inputs but reduces quality; stretching capacity strains staff.
Training costs more payroll but improves morale. Costs, loyalty, quality, regional
opportunity and staff conditions affect the actual annual profit.

Customer orders cost 1 time to accept and two further work stages that year.
They use regular sales capacity: annual revenue already includes their payment.
Reliable delivery improves loyalty; missed/reworked orders cause a remedial bill.
Delivery chances and order rolls are saved. A signed order locks its product.
Industry issues appear at most once a year and each authored issue appears once
per person. A depleted issue bank becomes quiet rather than recycling old scenes.

**Orders & accounts** records actual revenue, operating costs, wages, interest,
profit and the owner draw. Drawings/loss coverage also appear in the household
summary without being applied twice. **Family succession** lets you choose an
adult successor or hand ownership over now for 2 time and a local 500-base fee.
A child already running a company keeps it and cannot receive a second operating
company. A living handover gives no sale money and transfers debts, staff and
orders. Companies continue to earn or lose money off screen. After death, estate
obligations come first; a company may be sold if there is no eligible operator
or if the estate needs the proceeds. A retained company has one owner.


### Appearance

Open **Appearance** to choose the original skin tones, figure, hair, outfits,
background, rim and charm. Hair variants are free. The original portrait artwork
is restored. NPCs keep their own saved looks; switching to a child keeps theirs.
More facial-feature values remain saved, but their controls are hidden until they
can work within this original design. In-face expressions remain unfinished.
Optional themed looks and personal charms remain available in the Star Shop.
On screens with a brief summary, **Details** reveals the longer explanation inline.


### Stock and supply

**Business → Company operations → Stock & production** holds two product stocks.
Batches represent annual sales capacity; services use appointment/contract places.
The main line sells alone or takes 75% of demand in a two-line mix. Each lot keeps
its paid cost and age. Short stock loses sales; expired stock and unbooked
perishable/service capacity are written off. Customer orders reserve three batches.

Manual supply orders cost one time, once per product/year. Value suppliers take
two years (85% on time), reliable suppliers one year (97%), and specialists the
next accounts (99%). A late delivery moves the same saved contract one year.
Its bill is paid in its delivery-year cash accounts. Automatic replenishment targets
0/50/100/150 total batches after settlement, with the chosen product mix. Existing
contracts remain due when you change suppliers or turn automatic ordering off.

**Facilities** has two levels each of storage, production and research facilities.
Upgrades take two time, cost capital and add upkeep. **Market research** has two
saved assessments and a deadline; actual business skills and evidence affect the
outcome. Each successful insight adds 5% product demand, to three per product.

**Company cash** moves your own money into the reserve. Profit counts stock sold
and written off; cash flow pays supplier bills. Drawings need profit and cash;
you cover an actual cash shortfall. **Orders & accounts** keeps both views.
Selling transfers stock, staff and signed obligations to a living buyer. Closing
returns company reserve and 35% of physical stock's book cost, less 10% of signed
supplier bills and any unfinished customer's remedy fee. Borrowing is settled
separately by the existing business debt rules. No cash or stock is duplicated.

### Connections — v0.79

School projects, work, enterprise commitments and kept/broken promises can open
a named follow-up next year. Find Connections in More → Connected lives or the
related activity panel. One follow-up is available each year. Costs are shown;

Make a practical attempt, set an honest limit or take a risky shortcut. Readiness

Earned field support needs trust 60+, lasts two years and does not stack. Missing

mark and a brow scar. These are drawn onto the original face; other age/special
look extension coverage remains unfinished.

## Everyday responsibilities — v0.80

- **Teams & seasons:** choose Safe, Balanced or Push before a fixture. Safe uses
  less stamina; Push adds chance and strain. Existing injuries affect matches;
  recovery time restores stamina once a year and does not remove a diagnosis.
- **Work practice:** trade, interview and service challenges live under
  Occupation → Your job. Free practice gives no rewards; scored runs cost time
  and a small fee. Feedback appears with the relevant practice page.
- **Shared household reserve:** choose a monthly standing amount or pause it,
  and a cash floor. The game settles contributions annually from your own cash.
  The floor is fixed in cash when configured; it stays yours through a move.
  Shortfalls do not create savings debt. Separation pauses contributions; your
  old shares can still be returned. Partner money requires their existing consent.
- **Parenting & childcare:** open a child for an age-specific decision or yearly
  routine. Shared childcare needs an available adult helper. Fees/time are paid
  when arranged; next year's review supports the actual child. Renew yearly.
  A plan cannot change custody. Child development and upbringing memories remain
  with the child; switching viewpoint does not inherit a parent's obligations.
- **Health & support → Family caregiving:** arrange shared or professional respite
  on an existing current-year care schedule. It has a stated cost and one annual
  limit. Agreed cover reduces caregiver strain while remaining available.
- **Trouble & recovery:** a convicted person in custody may appeal once per case.
  Review the recorded hearing, then submit a reasoned request. The paid attempt
  and saved luck persist on reload; no purchase guarantees a reversal. A successful
  review reduces the remaining sentence without erasing the crime or verdict.
- **Places & community:** language practice is saved separately for each country.
  Local institutions need upkeep; their quality influences relevant opportunities.
  Maintenance costs time/money and is limited once yearly in your local community.

## More life, less clutter — v0.81

The original v0.81 life-activity categories were reorganized in v0.84. Current
pages group school and work, people and family, health, free time, money, recovery
and identity without separate Skills & competition or Growing up destinations.
Health labels distinguish appointments/family care, mood/grief/stress and
dependence/aftercare.

Event cards use a small icon beside the title and one rounded frame. Long scenes
and choices scroll inside the card; the life screen remains visible around it.
Minigame introductions, boards and results use the same card style. Enlarged
display settings retain extra board room. Pause/help and ending an attempt remain
available; the smaller card does not remove choices.

- **People & commitments → a person → Share a moment:** one time, once per person
  yearly. Three approaches have different learning, mood and effort trade-offs.
  The person's current priority affects trust. A next-year follow-up concludes
  the shared moment. People also progress and change priorities on their own.
- **Parenting & childcare → a child:** fourteen scenes in each of three age bands.
  Routines, flexibility and the child's own choice suit different preferences.
  Choices affect learning/mood and parental stress; memories and next-year outcomes
  stay with the child, including when continuing as that child.
- **Practical repair:** inspect three toy-console relays, change settings and test.
  An accurate repair with less wasted work scores better. Keys 1–3 change settings.
- **Music practice:** tap the displayed phrase in order, using buttons or keys 1–5.
  There is no timer and sound is optional. Clear lets you retry with a small score
  cost. Complete the phrase before submitting.
- **Contract negotiation:** adjust offer and delivery-scope sliders. Stay within
  the client's budget, cover cost and meet the required scope. Margin, delivery
  and room left for the client contribute to the score. Arrow keys adjust a
  focused slider.

New boards retain unfinished work on reload. Free practice has three rounds and
no rewards; scored attempts have five rounds, cost one time plus the listed fee,
and remain limited once yearly. Earlier saved workshop attempts keep their old
format. Use Enter to submit/continue; Tab moves between controls.
