# Roadmap

Priorities for a richer life simulation. These are proposals, not release promises.
The public branch is v0.25.0; v0.26 remains a separate local preview. Older plans
and their completion claims are preserved in [history](docs/history/ROADMAP.md).

## 1. Consequences that remain visible

Build on the existing flags, memories, follow-ups and Life Story. Connect a major
choice to later scenes, relationship reactions, work opportunities and the ending.
Example: covering for a coworker creates a debt, a later favour and a promotion
conflict, with different paths if trust is broken. Show why the consequence occurs.

**Success criterion:** each pilot arc survives save/load, offers distinct outcomes,
and can be encountered without forced repetition. Begin with a small set of arcs
across school, family and work before adding many more.

## 2. People with continuing ambitions

Extend the autonomous family and relationship systems with durable personal goals,
competing loyalties and remembered promises. Let a friend pursue education, leave
town, struggle or succeed while the player is busy. Relationships should affect
decisions through context, not only a closeness number.

**Success criterion:** the same NPC changes across several years, responds to a
specific past choice, and has a readable history that persists after reload.

## 3. Careers with a daily reality

Deepen ordinary jobs as well as special careers. Tie qualifications, mentors,
projects, mistakes, burnout and family commitments into career progression.
Make failure recoverable through retraining or another route.

**Success criterion:** two characters in the same job can have different career
stories; hiring and promotion explain relevant preparation and uncertainty.

## 4. Places that accumulate history

Extend local/world systems so changes reach rent, jobs, schools, safety and familiar
people. A closure or community project should be visible beyond its announcement.
Moving away should trade opportunities against lost proximity and support.

**Success criterion:** a persistent local change affects multiple systems and
remains coherent after a timeline, career or location change.

## 5. Identity, art and sound

Give the player a coherent layered avatar kit, readable mode emblems and a small
original icon family. Keep age and life circumstances visible. Add purposeful
musical variation and feedback without making repeated actions tiring.

**Success criterion:** a representative full life feels visually consistent,
works with reduced motion and large text, and has documented asset provenance.

## 6. Original story mode

For the safest long-term creative direction, build an original recurring cast
with distinct genres and settings. Keep ordered chapters, branching reflections
and clear arc endings. Resolve the rights questions for recognizable TVLife
characters before distributing them; see [release readiness](docs/RELEASE-READINESS.md).

**Success criterion:** a complete pilot story has its own identity, choices with
consequences, reliable checkpoints and multiple meaningful resolutions.

## How to decide what ships

Prefer a few connected, tested arcs to a large unconnected feature list. Observe
full playthroughs, collect repetitive or confusing moments, and verify that each
new system creates different life stories. Publish only after testing the actual
package, recording limitations and reconciling the credits and licence scope.
